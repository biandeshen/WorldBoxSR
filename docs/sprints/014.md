# Sprint 014 — Bracket deterministic grazer natural turnover

Status: **active** (#103)

## Goal

Resolve the remaining grazer life-cycle gap before default animals: age advances, reproduction can be enabled, and starvation regulates overload, but a well-fed low-pressure grazer currently has no natural death.

Do not choose one lifespan by taste. First measure simple deterministic individual lifespan bands as temporary research logic.

## Candidate mechanism

Each grazer receives a derived natural lifespan from keyed randomness over `world.seed + creature.id`. The probe stores no new authoritative lifespan field and consumes no sequential RNG.

Research senescence is applied after the day’s grazer action and day increment but before authoritative reproduction. A grazer at or beyond its derived lifespan is removed with a research-only old-age count, so it cannot reproduce after its natural-death age.

## Stage 1 — low-density bracket

Creature-only 24×24 worlds:

- landscape seeds `1/4/9`;
- 20 deterministic adult founders, age 2 years;
- authoritative reproduction explicitly enabled at `0.001`;
- 30-year horizon;
- keyed-uniform lifespan bands `8–12`, `12–18`, and `18–24` years.

Track living grazers, authoritative births, starvation deaths, research old-age deaths, founder survival, births after founders are gone, vegetation utilization/minimum, age distribution, yearly checkpoints, and sequential RNG isolation.

## Stage 1 decision

Reject a band if replacement cannot survive founder senescence or if the band produces no meaningful turnover within 30 years. Prefer the shortest band that demonstrates repeated generational replacement while keeping low-density vegetation resource-abundant.

Do not optimize toward a target population.

## Stage 2

Only the shortest plausible Stage-1 band advances to `100/200` founder carrying-pressure checks across seeds `1/4/9`, unchanged. It must preserve bounded local-resource dynamics and distinguish starvation mortality from old-age turnover.
