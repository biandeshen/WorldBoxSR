# Sprint 014 — Bracket deterministic grazer natural turnover

Status: **completed — negative runtime decision** (#103)

## Goal

Resolve the remaining grazer life-cycle gap before default animals: age advances, reproduction can be enabled, and starvation regulates overload, but a well-fed low-pressure grazer currently has no natural death.

Sprint 014 tested simple deterministic senescence as temporary research logic before introducing any runtime lifespan state.

## Candidate mechanism

Each grazer received a derived natural lifespan from keyed randomness over `world.seed + creature.id`. The probe stored no new authoritative lifespan field and consumed no sequential RNG.

Research senescence ran after the day’s grazer action and day increment but before authoritative reproduction.

## Stage 1 — low-density bracket

Twenty age-2 founders, seeds `1/4/9`, reproduction `0.001`, 30 years:

- `8–12`: all three worlds went extinct;
- `12–18`: seeds 1/4 finished with only 6/2 grazers;
- `18–24`: all three worlds survived complete founder turnover, finishing with 72/62/92 grazers and vegetation still at 64%–90%.

`18–24` was therefore the shortest low-density-plausible band.

## Stage 2 — carrying-pressure failure

The unchanged `18–24` band was tested at 100/200 founders against no-senescence controls.

Controls preserved the known resource-limited envelope. With senescence:

- 100 founders finished at `0 / 7 / 1`;
- 200 founders finished at `0 / 0 / 0`;
- vegetation returned to ~99%–100% capacity.

The mechanism therefore erased carrying-pressure persistence rather than adding healthy turnover.

## Stage 3 — wider lifespan windows

Holding the minimum at 18 years while widening to `18–30` and `18–36` over 45 years did not resolve the failure.

- `18–30`, 100 founders: `1 / 9 / 7` survivors;
- `18–36`, 100 founders: `7 / 33 / 3`;
- `18–30`, 200 founders: `3 / 5 / 4`;
- `18–36`, 200 founders: `16 / 19 / 9`.

Vegetation ended around 97%–100%. Lifespan parameter search was stopped rather than pushing the collapse farther into the future.

## Stage 4 — bottleneck diagnosis

The `18–36` case was instrumented without changing mechanics.

The failure is a two-stage recovery trap:

1. **resource recovery delay** — during pressure, mature/healthy/fed animals can still form 46–67 adjacent candidate pairs at sampled checkpoints, but the local vegetation >= 0.50 gate yields zero resource-eligible pairs; post-pressure resource-eligible pairs reappear only around years 24–28;
2. **spatial Allee effect** — by the time vegetation is abundant again, population has dispersed and collapsed. Most survivors are resource-ready, but after year34 the six diagnostic runs have only 0–7 resource-eligible adjacent pairs, usually 0–3.

The current reproduction gate is correctly suppressing births under resource collapse, but when natural mortality is layered on top, resource recovery and adjacent encounter opportunity do not overlap strongly enough to sustain replacement.

Full evidence: `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-natural-turnover.md`.

## Decision

**Do not add grazer old-age death yet.**

Simple keyed fixed-lifespan senescence is rejected for runtime at this gate. No lifespan field/config, old-age event, snapshot change, or default animal population is justified.

## Next gate

Keep birth chance and the local vegetation gate unchanged first. Research only the low-density reproductive encounter assumption: can slightly broader/local partner discovery remove the spatial Allee trap while leaving high-pressure resource suppression intact?

Change one causal assumption at a time. Do not compensate by tuning lifespan, birth probability, resource threshold, or a global population target.

## Cleanup

Temporary lifespan probes and CI artifact plumbing are removed before merge. Sprint 014 ships research evidence and a negative runtime decision only.
