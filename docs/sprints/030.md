# Sprint 030 — Fresh-validate encounter-safe compact grazer placement

Status: **complete — candidate rejected** (#136)

## Goal

Fresh-validate the exact frozen Sprint029 encounter-safe placement candidate on compact seeds not used in derivation.

## Fresh matrix

Seeds `61..120` × founders `2/4` × baseline/intervention = 240 worlds, year0→300. Algorithm, founder IDs/ages and all ecology were frozen.

## Result

### 2 founders

- baseline extinctions: **33**;
- intervention extinctions: **35**;
- rescued: **1**;
- harmed: **3**;
- zero-birth extinctions: 16→14;
- initial zero-pair worlds: 23→0.

### 4 founders

- baseline extinctions: **17**;
- intervention extinctions: **17**;
- rescued: **3**;
- harmed: **3**.

Combined rescued=`4`, harmed=`6`; the pre-registered `rescued > harmed` criterion fails, and founders2 extinction also worsens.

Harm audit shows no simple starvation tradeoff. Several harmed worlds retain abundant vegetation; seed68×4 intervention produces zero births and dies ~y26 with vegetation minimum ~76%, while its baseline survives y300. Initial graph connectivity is therefore not monotonically beneficial.

## Decision

**Reject the Sprint029 encounter-safe placement candidate.**

- no runtime/default-fauna promotion;
- no radius/top32/greedy/count/age/ecology tuning in this sprint;
- preserve both rescues and harms;
- future compact work must use a new causal hypothesis or reconsider automatic natural-fauna initialization on compact maps.

Permanent evidence: `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-encounter-safe-placement-fresh-validation.md`.
