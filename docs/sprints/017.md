# Sprint 017 — Re-evaluate grazer natural turnover with radius-3 encounters

Status: **completed — negative runtime gate** (#110)

## Goal

Re-run the natural-turnover gate after Sprint 016 corrected authoritative partner discovery from radius 1 to radius 3.

Sprint 014's lifespan results were not carried forward as final evidence because they were measured against the now-rejected radius-1 encounter geometry.

## Fixed authoritative reproduction

All probes used current runtime reproduction unchanged:

- partner-search Chebyshev radius 3;
- each parent's local vegetation test radius 1;
- explicit birth chance `0.001`;
- existing maturity, health, hunger, cooldown, stable-pairing, keyed-random, birth-event, and counter behavior;
- no sequential RNG.

## Research-only senescence

Each grazer received a derived keyed lifespan from `world.seed + creature.id`. No lifespan field/config/event/schema was added.

Temporary senescence ran after daily grazer action/day increment and before authoritative reproduction, so a grazer at its derived lifespan could not reproduce after its natural-death age.

## Stage 1 — low-density re-bracket

20 deterministic age-2 founders, seeds `1/4/9`, 30 years:

- `8–12y`: final **40 / 36 / 5**; seed9 collapses to a tiny non-recovering tail;
- `12–18y`: final **51 / 131 / 101** with continued post-founder/replacement reproduction on all three seeds;
- `18–24y`: final **71 / 136 / 92**, but seed9 records no births after complete founder disappearance.

Per the pre-registered rule, only the **shortest passing band, 12–18y**, advanced to Stage 2.

## Stage 2 — carrying-pressure validation

The unchanged 12–18y band ran for 60 years at 100/200 founders across seeds `1/4/9`.

### 100 founders

- seed1: **extinct** around year30;
- seed4: survives and slowly recovers to 42 after a minimum of 8;
- seed9: only **1** animal remains at year60.

### 200 founders

All three worlds go extinct around years **16–20**.

The mechanism is structural: high density first drives vegetation into the measured carrying-pressure regime, which correctly freezes reproduction. Founder senescence then starts before a sufficient replacement cohort exists. Vegetation recovers only after mortality has already reduced the population below a viable recovery stock.

## Exploratory 18–24 diagnostic

A post-hoc 18–24y carrying run was executed after the pre-registered Stage 2 failed. It is not an acceptance gate.

It also fails robustly:

- seed1 / 100 founders still goes extinct;
- all three 200-founder worlds still go extinct around years 23–26.

This rules out simply shifting the same hard six-year lifespan band later as an adequate fix. No further lifespan-band search is justified here.

## Decision

**Do not add authoritative grazer natural senescence.**

Radius 3 solved the low-density encounter geometry identified in Sprint 014, but a hard keyed-uniform lifespan band still creates a carrying-pressure turnover cliff.

The next mortality hypothesis must change the **shape of mortality** rather than keep tuning lifespan endpoints. A future research gate may compare a gradual age-dependent mortality hazard and/or realistic founder age structure while keeping reproduction/resource rules fixed.

Do not change birth chance, local vegetation threshold, encounter radius, or a population target to rescue mortality.
