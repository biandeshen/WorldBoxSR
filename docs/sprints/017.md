# Sprint 017 — Re-evaluate grazer natural turnover with radius-3 encounters

Status: **active** (#110)

## Goal

Re-run the natural-turnover gate after Sprint 016 corrected authoritative partner discovery from radius 1 to radius 3.

Sprint 014's lifespan results are no longer decisive because they were measured against the now-rejected radius-1 encounter geometry.

## Fixed authoritative reproduction

The probe uses the current runtime reproduction implementation unchanged:

- partner-search Chebyshev radius 3;
- each parent's local vegetation test radius 1;
- explicit birth chance `0.001`;
- existing maturity, health, hunger, cooldown, stable-pairing, keyed-random, birth-event, and counter behavior;
- no sequential RNG.

## Research-only senescence

Each grazer receives a derived keyed lifespan from `world.seed + creature.id`. No lifespan field/config is persisted.

Temporary senescence runs after daily grazer action/day increment and before authoritative reproduction. A grazer at its derived lifespan is removed as a research-only old-age death so it cannot reproduce after its natural-death age.

## Stage 1 — low-density re-bracket

Creature-only 24×24 worlds:

- seeds `1/4/9`;
- 20 deterministic age-2 founders;
- 30 years;
- keyed-uniform lifespan bands `8–12`, `12–18`, `18–24` years.

Track persistence through founder turnover, authoritative births, replacement-parent births, births after founders disappear, starvation deaths, research old-age deaths, vegetation/minimum, age structure, and sequential RNG.

Advance the **shortest** band that survives complete founder turnover on all three landscapes with continuing replacement-generation reproduction.

## Stage 2

Advance only that unchanged band to 100/200 founders across seeds `1/4/9` for multiple lifespan generations. It must remain bounded/resource-limited, persist on all landscapes, and keep starvation versus old-age mortality causally distinct.

Do not tune birth chance, encounter radius, local vegetation threshold, or a population target to rescue a failing lifespan band.
