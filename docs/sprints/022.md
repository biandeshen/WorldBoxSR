# Sprint 022 — Test whether natural grazer founders need map-size scaling

Status: **complete — fixed 10 rejected as universal; compact maps need a down-scaling gate** (#120)

## Goal

Test the simplest activation hypothesis first: the validated 10-grazer population might be a founder seed rather than a target density and therefore might not need to scale with map size.

Research-only. No runtime initializer or default-world change ships from this sprint.

## Fixed initializer and ecology

The exact Sprint 021 initializer remained unchanged:

- 10 founders;
- passable tiles sorted by initial vegetation descending, then y/x;
- round-robin placement over the 32 richest passable cells;
- keyed founder age in `[0,6y]` using the same initialization salt;
- normal grazer defaults otherwise.

Authoritative reproduction `0.001`, gradual old-age mortality, radius-3 partner discovery, radius-1 vegetation eligibility, hunger/starvation/movement/cooldown/resource semantics, and sequential-RNG isolation all remained fixed.

## Stage 1 — 60 years

Sizes `16×16 / 24×24 / 32×32 / 48×48` × seeds `1..12` = 48 worlds.

All **48/48** worlds pass. Fixed 10 therefore advanced unchanged under the pre-registered rule.

The screen already showed that large worlds do not require more founders: on 48×48, some landscapes were still resource-abundant at year60 while others had naturally expanded into carrying pressure from the same 10-founder seed.

## Stage 2 — 120 years

The universal fixed-10 hypothesis fails **3/48** worlds, all at `16×16`:

| Seed | Passable land | Year120 pop | Min after year20 | Births years101–120 | Final 5y mean vegetation |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 57 | **9** | 2 | 8 | ~74.2% |
| 6 | 66 | **5** | 5 | **3** | ~81.1% |
| 7 | 101 | **4** | 2 | **4** | ~99.0% |

All `24×24`, `32×32`, and `48×48` worlds pass the 120-year gate.

The failed compact worlds are not resource-collapsed. Vegetation is strongly recovered at the end, while population has fallen into sparse demographic/encounter tails. Seed7 is the clearest case: population is only 4 at both years110 and120 despite ~99% final-five-year vegetation utilization.

Full evidence: `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-founder-size-scaling.md`.

## Decision

**Fixed 10 founders are rejected as a universal 16–48 initializer.**

However, the failure is one-sided:

- 10 remains validated at 24×24;
- all tested 32×32 and 48×48 worlds also pass;
- large maps therefore show no evidence that founder count must scale upward;
- the unresolved problem is excessive initial density on some compact 16×16 / low-land landscapes.

Per the pre-registered guardrail, no alternate founder count or scaling formula is tested in Sprint 022.

## Next gate

Test one simple **down-only compact-map founder rule** while leaving the validated 24×24+ initializer at 10 founders.

The next hypothesis should be structural and pre-registered, not a parameter search over the three failed seeds. It must cover compact intermediate sizes and preserve the principle that founder count is an ignition seed, not a carrying-capacity controller.

Default worlds remain creature-free. Activation compatibility remains blocked until compact-map initialization is resolved.
