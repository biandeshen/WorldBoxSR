# Sprint 023 — Test down-only area scaling for compact grazer founders

Status: **complete — total-area down-scaling rejected** (#122)

## Goal

Test one structural compact-map founder-count rule after Sprint 022 showed fixed10 is reliable at 24×24+ but not universal at 16×16.

Research-only: no runtime initializer/default-fauna change.

## Pre-registered rule

`founders = min(10, max(2, floor(10 * width * height / (24 * 24))))`

This yields 4 founders at 16×16 and preserves 10 at 24×24+. No parameter was tuned after observation.

Placement/age semantics, reproduction `0.001`, gradual old-age mortality, radius-3 partner discovery, radius-1 vegetation eligibility, grazing/starvation/movement/cooldown semantics, and sequential-RNG isolation all remained unchanged.

## Stage 1 — 16×16, seeds1..30, 120 years

The 4-founder rule fails **3/30** worlds:

| Seed | Land cells | Final pop | Min after year20 | Births years101–120 | Result |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 2 | 57 | **9** | 9 | 8 | final-pop floor |
| 10 | 101 | **0** | 0 | **0** | extinction |
| 24 | 81 | 17 | 7 | **4** | final-window births |

Per the pre-registered rule, Stage 2 does **not** run.

The rule does repair two original Sprint 022 failures:

- seed6: fixed10 `5 / 3 final-window births` → 4 founders **12 / 10**;
- seed7: fixed10 `4 / 4` → 4 founders **32 / 27**.

But seed10 moves in the opposite direction: fixed10 passes, while 4 founders become extinct around year90 after the population falls `42 → 39 → 14 → 3 → 4 → 3 → 1 → 0` and vegetation fully recovers.

Seed7 and seed10 each have **101 passable land cells**, yet they prefer opposite founder-count directions. Total area is therefore insufficient, and passable-land count alone also cannot explain the response.

Full evidence: `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-compact-area-scaling.md`.

## Decision

**Reject total-area down-scaling.**

Do not try another formula immediately. The compact-world problem is non-monotonic and interacts with landscape/spatial/encounter structure and deterministic lineage trajectories.

## Next gate

Characterize compact-world founder-count sensitivity and initial spatial/topological descriptors before proposing another initializer rule.

Use a bounded response surface for diagnosis, not parameter-search-to-success. Determine whether any common robust founder count exists across compact landscapes and whether failure correlates with founder spatial coverage/land topology rather than scalar area.

Default worlds remain creature-free; activation remains blocked.
