# Sprint 029 — Test encounter-safe compact grazer founder placement

Status: **complete — candidate advances to fresh validation** (#134)

## Goal

Test one placement-only intervention for genuine compact founder encounter/establishment failures after scalar persistence gates were rejected.

## Fixed mechanics

16×16 worlds, founders `2/4`, identical founder IDs/ages, reproduction `0.001`, gradual old-age mortality, partner radius3, local vegetation radius1 threshold0.50, and all other ecology unchanged. Both arms use the same top32 vegetation-rich candidate pool; only selected coordinates differ.

## Matrix

Seeds `1..60` × founders `2/4` × baseline/encounter-safe = 240 worlds, year0→300.

## Result

### 2 founders

- baseline extinctions: **23**;
- encounter-safe extinctions: **22**;
- rescued: **1**;
- harmed: **0**;
- zero-birth extinctions: **6→5**;
- initial zero-pair worlds: **13→0**.

### 4 founders

- baseline extinctions: **13**;
- encounter-safe extinctions: **11**;
- rescued: **2**;
- harmed: **0**.

Median starvation deaths remain zero in both arms/counts. No systematic new resource-collapse tradeoff or paired harm was observed.

The three direct rescues are seed22×2, seed19×4 and seed33×4. Seed22×2 is the clearest establishment rescue: baseline initial pair graph disconnected and extinction ~y34 after 2 births; intervention connects the pair, produces 16 births by y20 / 366 total, and remains alive at y300.

The intervention does not guarantee establishment: several radius3-connected 2-founder worlds still produce zero births and die. Encounter connectivity is therefore one causal factor, not a survival controller.

## Decision

The exact frozen encounter-safe selection algorithm passes the pre-registered paired research gate and may proceed to **fresh-seed validation only**.

Do not tune radius3, top32 pool, counts, ages or ecology before validation. Do not promote to runtime/default fauna.

Permanent evidence: `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-encounter-safe-placement.md`.
