# Sprint 018 — Isolate founder-cohort synchronization in grazer mortality failure

Status: **complete — synchronization insufficient** (#112)

## Goal

Test whether Sprint 017's carrying-pressure extinction is primarily caused by an artificially synchronized founder cohort before inventing a different mortality curve.

This sprint is research-only. It adds no runtime senescence, lifespan state/config/events, snapshot changes, or reproduction/resource/encounter retuning.

## Fixed ecology

Authoritative behavior remained unchanged:

- grazer reproduction partner search radius 3;
- each parent's local vegetation measurement radius 1;
- local vegetation threshold 0.50;
- birth chance `0.001`;
- current maturity/health/hunger/cooldown/stable-pairing/keyed-random semantics;
- no sequential RNG;
- no global population target.

Research mortality remained the same rejected Sprint 017 hard keyed-uniform **12–18 year lifespan**.

## Single variable

Compared founder starting-age structure only:

1. **synchronized control** — every founder starts at exactly age 2;
2. **heterogeneous cohort** — founder age is deterministically/keyed over **0–6 years** from `world.seed + creatureId`.

The 0–6y range was pre-registered. No other founder-age range was searched.

## Matrix

- 24×24 creature-only worlds;
- seeds `1/4/9`;
- founder densities `100/200`;
- synchronized age2 vs heterogeneous age0–6;
- 60-year horizon;
- 30-day vegetation sampling;
- 5-year checkpoints.

Research ordering remained:

`environment regeneration → grazer action → day increment → research senescence → authoritative reproduction`

## Result

The synchronization hypothesis fails its acceptance gate.

### 100 founders, year 60

- age2 control: seed1 **0**, seed4 **42**, seed9 **1**;
- heterogeneous 0–6y: seed1 **1**, seed4 **130**, seed9 **16**.

Heterogeneity materially helps seed4 and increases replacement reproduction, but seed1 remains a one-animal tail and seed9 remains weak. This is not robust all-landscape persistence.

### 200 founders, year 60

Both cohorts go extinct on **all three seeds**.

The heterogeneous runs record zero births after complete founder disappearance and zero replacement-parent births. Their minimum vegetation utilization remains in the known high-pressure regime (~`1.75%–4.01%`).

Founder heterogeneity stretches complete founder turnover from roughly years `15.8–15.9` to `17.1–17.8`, but the extra 1–2 years do not build enough replacement stock before the hard death boundary removes the cohort.

Sequential world RNG remained unchanged in every run.

Full evidence: `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-founder-age-synchronization.md`.

## Decision

**Founder synchronization is not a sufficient explanation of the hard-lifespan failure.**

Therefore:

- stop founder-age range tuning;
- keep all tested hard keyed-uniform lifespan bands rejected;
- ship no grazer senescence from this sprint;
- keep reproduction/resource/encounter rules fixed;
- move the next mortality experiment to a genuinely different shape: **gradual age-dependent mortality hazard** rather than a keyed hard death day.

## Guardrails carried forward

The next mortality gate must not change birth chance, vegetation threshold, partner radius, condition gates, or add a population controller in the same experiment. It must preserve sequential RNG isolation and demonstrate multi-generation persistence at both low density and carrying pressure across all landscapes before any runtime mortality behavior is considered.
