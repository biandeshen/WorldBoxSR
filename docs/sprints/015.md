# Sprint 015 — Isolate low-density grazer reproductive encounter recovery

Status: **completed — radius 3 passes research gate** (#105)

## Goal

Resolve the low-density reproductive Allee effect diagnosed in Sprint 014 before revisiting natural senescence.

Sprint 014 showed a two-stage trap when mortality is layered onto radius-1 reproduction:

`resource depletion suppresses births → mortality reduces population → vegetation recovers → sparse spatial distribution suppresses adjacent encounters`

Sprint 015 keeps the local resource gate, birth chance, condition gates, cooldown, stable pairing, and keyed randomness unchanged and varies only partner encounter distance.

## Fixed reproduction rule

Temporary research reproduction preserves Sprint 012/013 semantics:

- age >= 1 world year;
- health >= 0.95;
- hunger <= existing grazer hungry threshold;
- each parent radius-1 local vegetation utilization >= 0.50;
- one-world-year successful-birth cooldown;
- keyed birth chance `0.001` per eligible pair/day;
- stable deterministic pair selection;
- one age-0 child on a parent tile;
- no sequential RNG.

A wider encounter radius changes partner discovery only. It does not move animals, change either parent's local resource test, create mate bonds, or imply a social/residential entity.

## Stage 1 — radius 1 / 2 / 3 under turnover stress

The rejected Sprint 014 keyed `18–36` year lifespan is reused only as a diagnostic stressor. Seeds `1/4/9`, founder densities `100/200`, 45 years.

Radius 1 reproduces the post-pressure Allee collapse. Radius 2 helps but is not robust: seed1 / 100 founders remains around 9–11 grazers through year45 despite nearly full vegetation.

Radius 3 is the smallest tested radius where all six stress worlds survive complete founder turnover and visibly recover:

- 100 founders: year45 `39 / 99 / 76`;
- 200 founders: year45 `42 / 61 / 70`.

Post-founder births are `29 / 74 / 58` and `28 / 42 / 50` respectively. The unchanged local vegetation gate still suppresses births during depletion.

## Stage 2 — 90-year multi-generation radius-3 validation

Radius 3 is held unchanged for 90 years with founders `20/100/200` across seeds `1/4/9`, still using the temporary `18–36` turnover stressor.

All 9 worlds persist through multiple lifespan generations:

| Founders | Seed 1 final | Seed 4 final | Seed 9 final |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 20 | 48 | 53 | 55 |
| 100 | 46 | 119 | 59 |
| 200 | 63 | 59 | 100 |

Replacement-generation parents account for most later births. The worlds show repeated bounded resource cycles rather than monotonic growth:

`vegetation abundant → births resume → population rises → vegetation falls → births stop → turnover lowers population → vegetation recovers`

Sequential `world.rng` remains unchanged.

## Stage 3 — no-senescence compatibility

Runtime still has no grazer senescence, so radius 1 and radius 3 were compared for 30 years without the temporary lifespan stressor.

### 100 founders

- radius 1: `114 / 155 / 130`;
- radius 3: `115 / 165 / 134`.

### 200 founders

- radius 1: `112 / 161 / 132`;
- radius 3: `113 / 168 / 130`.

Final vegetation remains around ~3%–6% in the overloaded regime. Radius 3 therefore preserves the same landscape-dependent carrying envelope.

Radius 3 does accelerate low-density approach: 20 founders reach `47/51/47` by year10 and `116/171/131` by year30, versus radius-1 `30/31/35` at year10. The same local resource gate still bounds growth; the change is timescale/encounter opportunity, not a population target.

Full evidence: `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-encounter-recovery.md`.

## Decision

**Radius 3 passes as the smallest reliable partner-search geometry.**

- radius 2 is rejected as insufficiently robust;
- radius 3 restores post-pressure reproduction across all tested landscapes;
- 90-year multi-generation runs remain bounded;
- the local resource gate remains causal;
- the current no-senescence carrying envelope remains intact;
- sequential RNG isolation remains exact.

This sprint ships evidence only. Runtime partner radius remains 1 until a separate implementation gate.

## Implementation handoff

Promote only the encounter geometry in a separate PR:

- maximum grazer reproduction partner Chebyshev distance `1 → 3`;
- local vegetation measurement stays radius 1;
- birth chance, maturity, health, hunger, cooldown, stable pairing, and keyed randomness stay unchanged;
- reproduction remains default-off and default worlds remain creature-free;
- add exact distance-3 success and distance-4 rejection tests;
- do not add public interaction-radius config/framework, mate bonds, sex, genealogy, migration, population targets, predators, or species infrastructure.

Only after radius 3 is authoritative should natural senescence be reintroduced and revalidated.
