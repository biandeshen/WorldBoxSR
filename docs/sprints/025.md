# Sprint 025 — Characterize cycle-aware persistence on compact grazer worlds

Status: **complete** (#126)

## Goal

Determine whether compact 16×16 worlds that fail a single year-120 terminal ecology gate are truly non-viable or merely sampled during a recoverable resource-demography trough.

Evaluation only: founder count/placement and runtime ecology remain unchanged.

## Pre-registered study

18 exact Sprint024 seed/count cases were run from year0 to year240 with 10-year checkpoints:

- 8 alive-at-year120 terminal-gate failures;
- 6 true-extinction controls;
- 4 year120 passing controls.

Tracked rolling20-year births, old-gate pass/fail, continuous population<10 and zero-birth spans, recoveries, extinction, vegetation, reproduction eligibility/pair opportunity, and sequential RNG.

## Result

The old terminal gate is strongly phase-sensitive:

- all **8/8** alive-at-year120 failures later pass the old gate again;
- their median gate flip count through year240 is **6**;
- one of those worlds (seed2×10) later goes extinct around year220.5 despite earlier recovery;
- one original passing control (seed2×8) also goes extinct around year144;
- all **6/6** true-extinction controls are extinct by year240 and **0/6** re-pass after year120.

Thus neither `fail at year120` nor `pass at year120` is a stable long-horizon persistence label.

Among the 10 worlds still alive at year240, the longest continuous population<10 span is ~**33.52 years** and longest continuous zero-birth span ~**24.18 years**. Every eventual extinction in this diagnostic set develops a much longer terminal stall; the shortest is seed2×10 at ~**52.59 years below10** and ~**48.62 years zero-birth**.

A **40-year recovery envelope** cleanly separates persistent vs eventual-extinction trajectories *in this diagnostic set*, but it is post-hoc/in-sample and is not accepted as a project gate.

## Decision

- retire the single-terminal-sample gate for compact initializer research;
- do not modify ecology, count, or placement from this sprint;
- treat 40 years only as a candidate recovery envelope;
- independently validate that candidate on unseen compact worlds before using it to judge initializer mechanics;
- only after validation should a spatial-seeding experiment target genuine establishment failures.

Permanent evidence: `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-cycle-aware-persistence.md`.
