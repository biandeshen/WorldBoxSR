# Sprint 012 — Test condition-gated grazer reproduction before runtime state

Status: **completed** (#97)

## Goal

Test one minimal reproduction mechanism as temporary research logic before adding birth/cooldown fields to authoritative creature state or advancing the snapshot schema.

Sprint 011 established a landscape-dependent no-reproduction carrying-pressure regime. Sprint 012 asks whether births can recover low-density populations while naturally suppressing themselves under resource pressure.

## Pre-registered mechanism

Research probe only:

- both grazers are at least 1 year old;
- Chebyshev distance <= 1;
- health >= 0.95 for both;
- hunger <= the existing grazer hungry threshold for both;
- radius-1 local vegetation utilization >= 0.50 for both;
- neither parent is inside a 360-day research-only birth cooldown;
- stable deterministic pair selection, at most one attempted pair per grazer/day;
- keyed-random birth chance 0.001 per eligible pair/day;
- successful birth creates one normal age-0 grazer on a parent tile and cools down both parents for 360 days.

No sex, persistent mate, genealogy, gestation, litter, species framework, or global population target is implied.

## Stage 1 — 10-year screen

Creature-only 24×24 worlds used landscape seeds `1/4/9`, deterministic adult starting densities `20/100/200`, and reproduction disabled/enabled pairs.

The mechanism immediately showed the desired direction:

- 20 founders grew to 30/31/35 with zero deaths and ~94%–97% final vegetation;
- 200 founders produced some early births, then resource pressure shut births down and final populations converged to 115/161/133 — essentially the no-reproduction 114/164/130 plateaus;
- 100 founders grew to 121/154/135, but seed1/9 vegetation approached ~5% by year10, so the horizon was extended without changing any rule parameter.

## Stage 2 — unchanged 20-year extension

Only 20/100 founder cases were extended to 20 years.

### 20 founders

Final populations were 65/64/78 with zero deaths and final vegetation utilization 80.2%/91.0%/82.0%. Low-density populations continue to expand gradually without resource destruction.

### 100 founders

Final populations were 115/155/134. Total births froze at 23/55/35; final vegetation utilization settled near 5.3%/5.3%/4.1%.

The temporal behavior is decisive: the local vegetation/condition gate naturally turns reproduction off as populations enter the resource-limited regime. Seed1 drifts from a maximum 121 to 115; seed9 remains ~134 after its last birth; seed4 stops at 155.

Every run preserved sequential world RNG after deterministic initialization.

Full evidence is recorded in `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-reproduction-probe.md`.

## Decision

**Pass the mechanism for a real off-by-default runtime implementation gate.**

The same pre-registered rule:

- expands low-density populations while vegetation remains abundant;
- suppresses births under resource/condition pressure;
- keeps overloaded worlds bounded;
- preserves landscape-dependent carrying pressure rather than imposing a target population;
- consumes no sequential world RNG.

## Handoff constraints

The runtime spike must:

- keep reproduction disabled by default;
- persist only the minimal cooldown state required;
- emit typed `creature.born` history with both parent creature IDs/references;
- preserve stable pairing and keyed randomness;
- prove disabled-mode behavior neutrality;
- preserve independent human/creature identity domains;
- avoid sex, mate-bond, genealogy, gestation, litter, predator, or generalized species infrastructure.
