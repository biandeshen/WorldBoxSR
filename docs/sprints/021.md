# Sprint 021 — Validate natural grazer initialization

Status: **complete — 10-founder 24×24 initializer passes broad research gate** (#118)

## Goal

Test whether a small deterministic founder population can turn the complete opt-in grazer mechanics into robust natural fauna across a broader landscape sample before ordinary generated worlds receive animals.

Research-only: no runtime initializer/default-world change ships from this sprint.

## Fixed authoritative ecology

- reproduction chance `0.001`;
- gradual old-age mortality enabled;
- partner-search radius 3;
- each parent's local vegetation radius 1 / threshold 0.50;
- current hunger, starvation, movement, cooldown, vegetation, and keyed-random semantics;
- no population target;
- no sequential RNG.

## Pre-registered initialization

- sort passable tiles by initial vegetation descending, then y/x;
- use the 32 richest passable cells;
- place founders round-robin across those cells;
- assign deterministic keyed age in `[0, 6 years]` from seed + future creature ID + a dedicated initialization salt;
- otherwise use normal grazer defaults.

## Stage 1 — founder-count selection

24×24 creature-only worlds, seeds `1..12`, counts `10 / 20 / 40`, 60 years.

All three counts pass every landscape. The pre-registered rule requires selecting the **smallest** all-seed candidate, so Stage 2 advances **10 founders**.

The weakest 10-founder Stage-1 world, seed7, reaches 9 after year20, ends year60 at 10, and still produces 10 births in the final 20 years. It is retained as evidence rather than used to justify changing the selected count.

## Stage 2 — broad validation

The exact 10-founder initializer runs unchanged on seeds `1..30` for 120 years.

All **30/30** worlds pass:

- no extinction;
- year120 population >=10;
- >=5 births in years101–120;
- replacement-parent reproduction present;
- maximum population <300;
- sequential RNG unchanged;
- no permanent vegetation collapse.

Across the 30 worlds:

- year120 population: min **15**, median **68**, max **166**;
- minimum post-year20 population: min **9**, median **22.5**;
- maximum observed population: max **167**;
- final-20-year births: min **7**, median **57**, max **159**.

Weak worlds remain cyclic rather than terminal: seed2 ends at 15 with 9 final-window births; seed7 recovers from 9 to 33; seed26 recovers from 9 to 35; seed29 has only 7 final-window births but remains at 30 with extensive replacement-parent reproduction.

Vegetation repeatedly depletes and recovers. Median minimum sampled utilization is ~**11.62%**; no world remains resource-collapsed. Only three starvation deaths occur across all 30 runs, so the 10-founder initializer does not begin by overloading the landscape.

Full evidence: `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-natural-grazer-initialization.md`.

## Decision

**The exact deterministic 10-founder initializer is ecologically credible for validated 24×24 worlds.**

This does not automatically make ordinary worlds fauna-on and does not prove that a fixed count of 10 is correct at other map sizes.

The next gate must resolve founder-count scaling and backward-compatible activation semantics before any runtime natural initializer is accepted.

No predators, second species, human/settlement interaction, art work, or ecology retuning is justified by Sprint 021.
