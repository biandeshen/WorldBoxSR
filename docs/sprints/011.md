# Sprint 011 — Measure grazer carrying pressure before reproduction

Status: **completed** (#95)

## Goal

Characterize long-horizon survival and vegetation pressure across explicit grazer densities before adding any reproduction rule or default animals.

Sprint 010 proved the one-way loop:

`vegetation regrowth → grazer grazing → biomass depletion → regrowth`

Sprint 011 asks whether that loop exposes a legible carrying-pressure regime strong enough to constrain a future reproduction experiment.

## Study

Creature-only 24×24 worlds used default grazer parameters, no humans, deterministic placement across vegetation-rich land cells, and a strict sequential-RNG isolation assertion.

### Stage 1 — broad bracket

- landscape seeds `1/4/9`;
- initial densities `5/20/100/300`;
- 5-year horizon;
- checkpoints at years `1/2/3/5`;
- vegetation sampled every 30 days.

Stage 1 found a clear transition: `5/20` were resource-light, `100` applied substantial but mostly survivable pressure, and `300` produced widespread starvation plus near-exhausted vegetation.

### Stage 2 — transition refinement

The transition was refined over 10 years with densities `100/150/200/250/300` on the same three landscape seeds.

At year 10:

| Initial grazers | Seed 1 survivors | Seed 4 survivors | Seed 9 survivors |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 | 96 | 100 | 100 |
| 150 | 113 | 150 | 132 |
| 200 | 114 | 164 | 130 |
| 250 | 114 | 165 | 130 |
| 300 | 115 | 165 | 127 |

For initial densities `>=200`, each landscape converges toward a nearly stable survivor scale:

- seed 1: ~114–115;
- seed 4: ~164–165;
- seed 9: ~127–130.

Final vegetation utilization in those overloaded runs remains around ~2%–6%, while `100` grazers retain ~22.8%–62.7% depending on landscape. `150` is the transition band.

Full evidence is recorded in `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-carrying-pressure.md`.

## Decision

**Pass the gate for a narrow off-by-default reproduction experiment.**

The no-reproduction system exposes a clear resource-limited regime and landscape-dependent carrying pressure. Do not retune grazer energy/resource scale before testing births.

This is not approval for default animals, predators, a species framework, or a global population controller.

## Constraints handed to the next sprint

Any reproduction spike must:

- remain off by default;
- use individual condition plus local vegetation rather than a global target;
- stop producing births under hunger/starvation/resource collapse;
- use keyed randomness/stable ordering so sequential human RNG remains untouched;
- preserve independent human/creature identity domains;
- remain bounded across multiple landscapes and starting densities;
- recover low-density populations without permanently flattening vegetation.
