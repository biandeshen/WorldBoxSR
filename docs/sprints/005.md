# Sprint 005 — Explain settlement demographic viability

Status: **completed — natural extinction explained; no rescue behavior justified** (#83 / #84)

## Goal

Explain **how settlements replace or lose members over time** before adding any persistence, migration, fertility, household, or economy behavior.

The sprint is derived-only and changes no simulation behavior.

## Instrumentation

### Demographic stock

At a 30-day research cadence, every active settlement exposes:

- population and sex counts;
- minors/adults and female/male minors;
- reproductive-age females using existing adult/fertility bounds;
- descriptive same-age-band males and later adults;
- **female replacement pipeline = female minors + reproductive-age females**;
- exact authoritative eligible-female/eligible-male counts;
- eligible females with/without an eligible male in the existing Chebyshev-radius-1 reproduction neighborhood;
- compact age statistics.

No male fertility-end threshold is invented; authoritative male eligibility has none.

### Exact sampled member flow

Stable human IDs reconcile every 30-day settlement stock change into:

- surviving newborn additions;
- external-spawn additions;
- entries from no settlement;
- switches in;
- deaths;
- exits to no settlement;
- switches out.

Natural birth production is also annotated to the mother's prior-sample settlement so "birth produced here" is not conflated with "newborn first sampled here."

Every research interval must satisfy exactly:

`population delta = additions + inflow - deaths - outflow`

All long-run probes completed with **zero reconciliation error** and no tracker-cap truncation.

## Six-seed year100 → year200 cohort

Seeds `1/4/9/45/80/98` produced 41 active year100 settlements:

- 1 later abandoned;
- 5 remained active but declined;
- 35 were stable/growing.

Median second-century stock decomposition:

| Outcome | Count | Pop Δ | Surviving newborns | Deaths | Natural stock balance | Non-death membership balance |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| abandoned | 1 | **-11** | 0 | 13 | **-13** | **+2** |
| active decline | 5 | -5 | 397 | 376 | **+21** | **-25** |
| stable/growing | 35 | +36 | 466 | 428 | **+38** | **+4** |

There is no single "settlement decline" mechanism. The active-but-declining seed98 settlements still replace mortality naturally; their decline is mainly membership redistribution. The true abandonment is the opposite: membership balance is slightly positive, but natural replacement collapses.

## Pineford vs close-size recovery

### Seed98 Pineford (#5)

Year100 population 11 → 0:

- surviving newborn additions: 0;
- deaths: 13;
- non-death membership balance: +2;
- exact change: `0 - 13 + 2 = -11`.

At year100 Pineford already has:

- 0 female minors;
- 0 reproductive-age females;
- 2 later-adult females;
- 7 eligible males;
- 0 eligible females.

By year120 there are no females at all. By year140 all remaining humans are older males.

Its female replacement pipeline first reaches zero around year63 and remains continuously absent for roughly 86 observed years before abandonment.

### Automatically selected close-size recovery

Seed80 Briarmere (#2), year100 population 10 → year200 population 93:

- 1 reproductive-age female at year100;
- 100% local eligible-male opportunity for that eligible female;
- surviving newborn additions 284;
- deaths 152;
- natural stock balance +132;
- non-death membership balance -49;
- exact population change +83.

Strong replacement can outweigh substantial member leakage; Pineford has the mirror failure.

## All known natural abandonments

The existing post-social 100×200 baseline has only **three worlds with any natural settlement abandonment**: seeds 49, 62, and 98, one each. Sprint 005 audited all three.

Across the 3 abandoned settlements:

- median share of active samples with no reproductive-age female: **65.53%**;
- median share with female replacement pipeline = 0: **62.65%**;
- median longest observed zero-pipeline span: **25,290 days** (~70.3 years).

Across 25 settlements still active in those same worlds:

- median no-reproductive-age-female share: **3.76%**;
- median zero-pipeline share: **0.51%**;
- median longest zero-pipeline span: **60 days**.

Persistent pipeline absence is therefore strongly enriched among natural abandonment, but the three causal paths are heterogeneous.

### Seed49 Briarfield

- female pipeline absent from founding for 100% of active samples;
- 0 surviving newborns / 0 births produced;
- 5 entrants, 5 deaths, no outflow;
- clean no-replacement extinction.

### Seed62 Aldervale

- very early abandonment around year15;
- female pipeline zero for the final ~31% of active samples;
- 42 entrants + 1 newborn, 43 exits, 0 deaths;
- immediate cause is membership outflow; pipeline loss is terminal fragility, not the initial driver.

### Seed98 Pineford

- long-lived settlement later loses its entire female replacement pipeline;
- then ages into an all-male / later all-old-male population;
- replacement collapses while non-death membership remains roughly balanced.

## Decision

### Keep the derived demographic/flow instruments

They provide exact, auditable settlement stories without adding authoritative state or behavior.

### Do not add a persistence/rescue rule

Natural abandonment is rare (**3/100 worlds by year200**) and causally heterogeneous. A fertility bonus, migration attraction, member lock, household rescue, or special persistence mechanic would script away legitimate emergence and risks flattening the world.

**Settlement extinction remains valid world history.**
