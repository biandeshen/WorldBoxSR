# Sprint 005 — Explain settlement demographic viability

Status: **active** (#83)

## Goal

Explain **how settlements replace or lose members over time** before adding any persistence, migration, fertility, household, or economy behavior.

The sprint is derived-only. It should turn population change from a single outcome number into auditable demographic and membership flows.

## Why now

Sprint 004 established two important facts:

1. genuine territorial scarcity is common in dense settlements;
2. scarcity is not what kills the known seed98 Pineford sentinel.

Pineford declines from `11 → 8 → 7 → 3 → 0` while territorial meal coverage rises from ~21 to ~80 meals per member and while the scarcity tracker records **zero territorial shortage, zero local blockage, and zero access mismatch**.

The unresolved mechanism is therefore member replacement, not food buffering.

## Observation model

At a fixed 30-day cadence, derive for each active settlement:

### Population stock

- total population;
- minors and adults using the existing `adultAgeYears`;
- reproductive-age females using existing adult/fertility bounds;
- adult males;
- currently reproduction-eligible females and males using existing hunger/cooldown semantics;
- dependent/adult ratio;
- compact age distribution.

No new demographic thresholds should be invented unless directly tied to existing simulation rules.

### Local reproduction opportunity

For each currently eligible female assigned to the settlement, ask whether at least one currently eligible male exists within the existing reproduction neighborhood, Chebyshev radius 1.

Report:

- eligible females;
- eligible females with local eligible-male opportunity;
- eligible females without local opportunity;
- opportunity coverage share.

This is observation only. Do not change partner selection, fertility, movement, or settlement membership.

### Membership flow

Using stable human IDs and sampled `settlementId` transitions, distinguish:

- entrant from no settlement;
- entrant from another settlement;
- exit to no settlement;
- direct switch to another settlement;
- death of a previously sampled member;
- newly observed newborn/child additions with explicit accounting semantics.

Death must not also be counted as a generic exit.

### Replacement accounting

For each sampled interval expose components rather than a synthetic score:

- births/new member additions vs deaths;
- non-death inflow vs non-death outflow;
- observed population delta;
- reconciliation error, which should be zero under the documented sampling semantics;
- sustained negative natural replacement vs sustained membership leakage.

## Longitudinal questions

Use bounded episodes/windows for transparent conditions such as:

- eligible females present but zero local reproduction opportunity;
- long periods without birth/new young replacement;
- sustained non-death net outflow;
- sustained deaths exceeding births;
- changing age structure as population approaches extinction.

Do not invent a black-box viability score or classifier.

## Sentinel and comparison

### Seed98 Pineford (#5)

Decompose the decline from year100 to abandonment:

- how many members die;
- how many leave or switch settlement;
- how many enter;
- how many births/new young replacements occur;
- how reproductive-age structure and local opportunity change.

### Recovery comparison

Choose at least one comparably small settlement that later grows or recovers. Compare the same flow components so Pineford is not explained by post-hoc storytelling.

## Baseline

At minimum seeds `1/4/9/45/80/98`, `24×24`, 30 founders, 200 years if runtime remains acceptable.

Use the year100 cohort and classify only by observed outcome for analysis:

- later abandoned;
- still active but declined;
- stable/growing.

The outcome labels are research grouping only, not authoritative state.

## Decision gate

A future settlement-persistence mechanic is worth designing only if a recurring, causal-looking failure mode appears across multiple settlements/seeds, for example:

- chronic reproduction-opportunity failure;
- systematic membership leakage/capture;
- mortality exceeding replacement for sustained periods.

If Pineford is an idiosyncratic but internally coherent extinction path, preserve it as legitimate emergence rather than adding a rescue rule.

## Non-goals

- no storage/economy retry;
- no fertility bonus;
- no migration or home-bias change;
- no household/spouse/social-bond identity;
- no resource sharing;
- no jobs/buildings/trade/tax/currency;
- no kingdoms/war;
- no client polish unless necessary for research inspection.

## Quality gates

- exact snapshot/RNG neutrality;
- tracker state external to authoritative world state;
- bounded memory, no raw daily trajectories;
- flow accounting reconciles sampled population change exactly under explicit semantics;
- deterministic artificial-world tests for death vs exit, direct switching, entry, newborn addition, and reproduction-opportunity coverage;
- temporary expensive probes removed before merge;
- negative results preserved instead of tuned away.
