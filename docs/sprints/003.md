# Sprint 003 — Settlements buffer scarcity

## Goal

Test whether a **small deterministic shared food store** lets settlements buffer short local shortages without turning food into a universal survival bonus or flattening world diversity.

This is a controlled causal experiment, not the start of a general economy system.

## Why now

Territory/resource accounting already showed that settlement food conditions contain real signal for later growth:

- food remaining fraction correlated 0.780 with year-100 → year-200 absolute growth across 37 targeted settlements;
- the correlation rose to 0.864 when the known sparse seed45 sentinel was excluded;
- per-member territorial resource metrics were similarly predictive outside seed45.

But resource abundance is not sufficient for survival: seed98 settlement #5 had ~96% food remaining at year100 and still disappeared by year200.

The project has also now ruled out several tempting but weak social shortcuts: lineage is not household, co-parent history is not spouse state, top dyads turn over, and radius-1 peer sets are a fluid local social field rather than a stable residential roster.

The next highest-value question is therefore settlement-level **scarcity buffering**, not another identity layer.

## Causal hypothesis

A bounded settlement store may improve outcomes specifically when:

1. territory has generated surplus during better periods;
2. current local tile food is temporarily scarce;
3. an active settlement member would otherwise fail to eat locally.

If storage instead causes broad population inflation, saves demographically nonviable settlements regardless of scarcity, or removes meaningful settlement diversity, the mechanic is rejected.

## Execution order

1. **Storage state + exact disabled-path guard**
   - add the smallest serializable settlement storage field/state needed for the experiment;
   - configuration defaults off;
   - disabled worlds must remain byte-identical in authoritative state/RNG behavior to the pre-storage model.

2. **Bounded surplus inflow**
   - define an explicit deterministic inflow rule from existing territorial food surplus;
   - impose a hard capacity;
   - no production bonus and no new food creation beyond whatever transfer rule is explicitly modeled.

3. **Scarcity-only consumption**
   - storage may be used only by current members when ordinary local eating fails under clearly defined scarcity;
   - no movement preference, job, household, class, or individual inventory system.

4. **Instrumentation**
   - store level / capacity;
   - deposits and withdrawals;
   - meals supplied by store;
   - wasted/blocked surplus if relevant;
   - settlement scarcity exposure before/after storage;
   - no hidden mutation inside derived metrics.

5. **A/B evidence gate**
   - storage off vs on across multiple seeds/horizons;
   - include ordinary seeds plus seed45 and seed98;
   - compare population distribution, settlement survival/abandonment, food pressure, territory use, and diversity rather than one success seed.

## Hard guards

### Seed45

Storage must not become a second demographic amplifier on top of settlement cohesion. If the sparse sentinel is pushed into another 600–800 population overcorrection regime, reject or redesign the mechanic.

### Seed98

Storage must not magically preserve settlements whose failure is primarily demographic/spatial rather than resource-driven. Abundant territory without viable social/demographic structure must remain capable of dying out.

## Non-goals

- jobs/professions;
- buildings/granaries;
- trade routes or markets;
- taxation/currency;
- production multipliers;
- household/private inventory;
- inheritance/classes;
- culture/religion/technology;
- kingdoms/rulers/diplomacy/war;
- client polish unless required to inspect the experiment.

## Quality gates

- deterministic replay and save/load remain exact;
- storage-disabled path preserves legacy behavior and sequential RNG path;
- no extra random draw simply because storage is enabled/disabled;
- resource transfer is conserved and auditable;
- all new counters/metrics have unit tests;
- no default-on behavior until multi-seed A/B evidence passes;
- performance is measured before optimizing or introducing more complex economy architecture.

## Stop criteria

Reject the storage direction rather than expanding it if any of these hold:

- only narrow hand-picked seeds improve;
- population center shifts materially upward without an interpretable scarcity mechanism;
- abandonment diversity collapses;
- seed45 or other sparse worlds over-amplify;
- seed98-like demographically dead settlements are rescued merely by resource abundance;
- the result requires trade/jobs/buildings to become legible before the basic storage mechanism proves itself.
