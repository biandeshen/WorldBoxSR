# Sprint 027 — Validate sustained zero-birth stall as compact non-recovery signal

Status: **complete — specificity pass, sensitivity unresolved** (#130)

## Goal

Test whether sustained generational inactivity—continuous absence of grazer births—is a useful compact-world non-recovery signal after Sprint026 falsified low population duration as a trigger.

Evaluation only: runtime ecology, founder placement/count semantics, and default-world behavior remain unchanged.

## Fresh validation

Seeds `31..60` × founders `2/4/6/8/10` = **150 worlds**, year0→300.

After year20, the exact pre-registered rule flags a still-living world only after **40 continuous years with zero births**. Population size is descriptive only. Any later checkpoint with rolling20 births>=5 is a false non-recovery signal.

## Result

- 150 worlds;
- 32 extinctions by year300;
- still-living worlds reaching 40 zero-birth years: **0**;
- false signals: **0**;
- censored flagged worlds: **0**.

The pre-registered zero-false-signal criterion therefore passes, but the rule never fires while a world is alive.

Among the 118 year300 survivors, the longest zero-birth span is only ~**22.71 years**. All 32 eventual extinctions occur before reaching 40 continuous zero-birth years while still alive.

## Decision

- record a **specificity/no-false-signal pass** on 150 fresh worlds;
- do **not** promote 40-year zero-birth as a complete compact persistence gate because it has zero observed pre-extinction sensitivity;
- do not shorten 40 years or add population/resource conditions in this sprint;
- do not alter ecology/count/placement;
- next research must characterize terminal pre-extinction birth-stall duration separately, derive any shorter candidate transparently, and validate it later on another fresh seed set.

Permanent evidence: `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-zero-birth-stall-validation.md`.

Temporary probe/test override are removed before merge. Default worlds remain creature-free.
