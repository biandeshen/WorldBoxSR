# Sprint 026 — Validate compact 40-year recovery envelope on unseen cases

Status: **complete — candidate rejected** (#128)

## Goal

Independently test the exact 40-year recovery-envelope candidate from Sprint025 on compact worlds outside the 18-case derivation set. Evaluation only; runtime ecology and initialization remain unchanged.

## Pre-registered validation

Seeds `17..30` × founders `2/4/6/8/10` = **70 worlds**, year0→300.

The candidate was fixed before results:

- after year20, flag a still-living world when either continuous `population <10` or continuous zero births reaches **40 years**;
- any later checkpoint with population>=10 and rolling20 births>=5 is a false non-persistence signal;
- any false signal rejects the candidate;
- no threshold changes allowed.

## Result

- 70 worlds;
- 11 extinctions by year300;
- 2 worlds crossed the candidate boundary while alive;
- **1 false non-persistence signal**;
- 0 censored flagged/alive/unrecovered worlds.

The decisive counterexample is **seed20 × 2 founders**:

- flagged around year59.997 after ~40 years below population10;
- meaningful recovery at year70: population14 and rolling20 births14;
- final year300 population36;
- longest low-pop span ~41.81y;
- longest zero-birth span only ~14.81y.

Thus a compact world can remain numerically small for >40 years while retaining enough generational activity to recover.

The other flagged case, seed18×10, goes extinct around year297 and is compatible with the rule, but cannot rescue a rule already falsified.

## Decision

**Reject the 40-year `low population OR zero births` envelope.**

- do not change 40 to another duration here;
- do not silently switch `OR`→`AND`;
- do not silently switch to birth-only logic;
- do not change ecology/count/placement;
- preserve seed20×2 as falsifying evidence.

The next evaluation hypothesis should isolate **generational/reproduction stall** from low abundance in a separate pre-registered issue and new validation set.

Permanent evidence: `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-recovery-envelope-unseen-validation.md`.

Temporary validation probe/test override are removed before merge. Default worlds remain creature-free.
