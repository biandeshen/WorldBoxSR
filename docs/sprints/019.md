# Sprint 019 — Test gradual age-dependent grazer mortality hazard

Status: **complete — candidate passes 120-year research gate** (#114)

## Goal

Test one gradual mortality shape after Sprints 017–018 showed that neither hard keyed lifespan bands nor founder-age heterogeneity can robustly preserve grazer turnover at carrying pressure.

Research-only: no runtime old-age death/config/state/event/schema change in this sprint.

## Fixed authoritative ecology

Unchanged throughout:

- reproduction partner search radius 3;
- each parent's local vegetation radius 1 with threshold 0.50;
- birth chance `0.001`;
- maturity / health / hunger / one-year successful-birth cooldown / stable pairing;
- keyed randomness and separate creature identity;
- no global population target;
- no sequential RNG.

All founders start at age 2 so mortality **shape** is the only changed causal variable relative to Sprint 017.

## Pre-registered hazard

No research old-age death before age 12.

For age >= 12:

`pAnnual(age) = min(0.50, 0.01 * 2 ^ ((ageYears - 12) / 3))`

Daily conversion:

`pDaily = 1 - (1 - pAnnual) ^ (1 / daysPerYear)`

A keyed daily chance from `(world.seed, creature.id, world.day, dedicated salt)` runs after grazer action/day increment and before authoritative reproduction.

Reference annual rates: age12 1%, age15 2%, age18 4%, age21 8%, age24 16%, age27 32%, age30+ 50% cap. There is no hard maximum age.

No curve parameter was tuned after observation.

## Stage 1 — 60 years

Seeds `1/4/9` × age-2 founders `20/100/200` all remained multi-generational. Every run retained replacement-parent births and nonzero births in the final 20 years, so the unchanged curve advanced to Stage 2.

The weakest screen case, seed1/100, reached 5 animals but ended at 11 and still produced 12 births in years41–60. It was a recovery-capable low, not a terminal tail.

## Stage 2 — 120 years

All 9 worlds remain alive and reproducing.

Final populations:

| Founders | seed1 | seed4 | seed9 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 20 | 47 | 104 | 72 |
| 100 | 91 | 119 | 52 |
| 200 | 102 | 134 | 86 |

Births in the final 20 years are positive in all 9 runs: `24/73/53`, `89/90/16`, and `99/116/72` for founder densities `20/100/200` respectively.

Minimum post-year20 populations remain finite but recovery-capable: `32/43/20`, `5/10/14`, and `5/7/13`. The high-density seed1 worlds recover from minima of 5 to year120 populations of 91 and 102.

Vegetation still enters the known carrying-pressure range: 100-founder minima are ~`3.8%–5.9%`; 200-founder minima are ~`1.9%–3.5%`. Population and vegetation then cycle rather than converge to a scripted target.

Old-age deaths are distributed broadly. Median death age is roughly 23.5–24.2 years across runs; observed maxima reach ~32.7–36.8 years. The hazard does not recreate a hidden hard cutoff.

Sequential world RNG remains exactly unchanged.

Full evidence: `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-gradual-mortality-hazard.md`.

## Decision

**The exact pre-registered gradual hazard passes the research gate.**

It is eligible for a separate authoritative implementation spike, but no mortality code ships from this research sprint.

The implementation gate must:

- promote the exact curve without retuning;
- be default-off so existing worlds remain behavior-identical;
- use keyed randomness only;
- route old-age removal through typed `creature.died` history with cause `old_age`;
- preserve starvation as a distinct cause;
- preserve deterministic save/load and sequential RNG isolation;
- re-run multi-seed low-density and carrying-pressure ecology evidence.

No population target, lifespan endpoint, founder-age tuning, reproduction/resource retuning, predator/species framework, or social/mate state is justified by Sprint 019.
