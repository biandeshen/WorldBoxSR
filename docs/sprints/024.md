# Sprint 024 — Diagnose compact grazer founder-count and spatial sensitivity

Status: **complete** (#124)

## Goal

Stop proposing scalar scaling formulas and diagnose why compact 16×16 worlds respond non-monotonically to founder count.

Research-only: no founder count, formula, placement, runtime initializer, or default-fauna behavior is promoted from this sprint.

## Fixed mechanics

Use 16×16 creature-only worlds and the exact Sprint 021 vegetation-rich placement/age family. Keep reproduction `0.001`, gradual old-age mortality, partner radius3, local vegetation radius1/threshold0.50, all grazing/starvation/movement/cooldown semantics, and sequential-RNG isolation unchanged.

## Stage 1 — bounded response surface

Seeds `1..30` × founder counts `2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10` × 120 years = 150 worlds.

Pass counts:

- 2 founders: 23/30;
- 4 founders: 27/30;
- 6 founders: 26/30;
- 8 founders: 26/30;
- 10 founders: 22/30.

No count passes all 30 seeds. Count response is strongly non-monotonic and static land/founder descriptors do not cleanly separate pass/fail outcomes.

## Stage 2 — trajectory diagnosis

The first ten seed numbers whose pass/fail status changes across counts were re-run with 10-year checkpoints:

`2 / 4 / 6 / 7 / 9 / 10 / 13 / 14 / 15 / 16`.

The trajectories separate three phenomena:

1. **establishment/encounter failure** — very small founder sets can remain resource-rich but fail to form usable radius-3 reproductive encounters before old age removes them;
2. **post-establishment resource-demography failure** — some fully connected founder sets grow successfully, enter resource suppression, then fail to rebuild reproductive stock after old-age turnover;
3. **terminal phase sensitivity** — several year-120 gate failures are still alive with recovered vegetation and viable reproductive pair opportunity, so the terminal sample is not equivalent to irreversible failure.

## Decision

- reject a universal scalar founder count;
- keep total-area/passable-land scaling rejected;
- do not promote a static topology threshold or new placement rule from this evidence;
- do not tune ecology to rescue compact cases;
- do not use the current year-120 terminal gate to choose initializer mechanics until long-cycle phase sensitivity is characterized;
- keep default worlds creature-free.

The next gate should be **cycle-aware persistence validation**: use longer-horizon/rolling-window trajectory evidence to distinguish true extinction/non-recovery from a recoverable demographic trough. Only after that should a separate spatial-seeding experiment be considered.
