# Sprint 024 — Diagnose compact grazer founder-count and spatial sensitivity

Status: **active — Stage 2** (#124)

## Goal

Stop proposing scalar scaling formulas and diagnose why compact 16×16 worlds respond non-monotonically to founder count.

Research-only: no founder count, formula, placement, runtime initializer, or default-fauna behavior can be promoted from this sprint.

## Fixed mechanics

Use 16×16 creature-only worlds and the exact Sprint 021 vegetation-rich placement/age family. Keep reproduction `0.001`, gradual old-age mortality, partner radius3, local vegetation radius1/threshold0.50, all grazing/starvation/movement/cooldown semantics, and sequential-RNG isolation unchanged.

## Stage 1 — completed bounded response surface

Seeds `1..30` × founder counts `2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10` × 120 years = 150 worlds.

Pass counts were:

- 2 founders: 23/30;
- 4 founders: 27/30;
- 6 founders: 26/30;
- 8 founders: 26/30;
- 10 founders: 22/30.

No count passes all 30 seeds. Scalar founder count is therefore insufficient.

The response is strongly non-monotonic in multiple seeds, including seed2 (`10010`), seed10 (`10111`), seed16 (`11011`), seed24 (`10101`), seed26 (`11010`), and seed29 (`01110`). Static land/founder topology descriptors do not cleanly separate pass/fail outcomes.

Permanent Stage-1 evidence is recorded in `docs/experiments/2026-08-24-grazer-compact-founder-sensitivity.md`.

## Stage 2 — active trajectory diagnostic

Because Stage 1 remains causally ambiguous, use the pre-registered deterministic selection: the first ten seed numbers whose pass/fail status changes across the five counts:

`2 / 4 / 6 / 7 / 9 / 10 / 13 / 14 / 15 / 16`.

Run only these 10 seeds × `2/4/6/8/10` founders × 120 years = 50 worlds. Do not change counts or mechanics.

Collect 10-year checkpoints for:

- living population and founders alive;
- cumulative births;
- starvation vs old-age deaths;
- vegetation utilization;
- mean hunger;
- reproduction-eligible grazers;
- radius-3 pair edges among reproduction-eligible grazers;
- occupied cells and mean age.

The purpose is to distinguish:

1. failure to establish an initial reproductive cohort;
2. resource suppression followed by unsuccessful demographic recovery;
3. encounter/pair collapse despite recovered resources;
4. mortality timing that removes the remaining reproductive stock;
5. ordinary long cycles that merely look weak at the year-120 terminal sample.

## Decision rule

Do not promote a new initializer in this sprint.

Use Stage 2 only to choose the **next single causal hypothesis**. If failures consistently show reproductive encounter collapse while resource/health eligibility is available, a spatial founder-placement experiment is justified. If failures instead arise after resource pressure/demographic turnover despite adequate pair opportunity, do not alter placement merely to rescue outcomes.

No additional founder counts, ecology tuning, runtime initialization, or default fauna are allowed here.
