# v0.6 backlog — Living Ecology

Release gate: #223. Version promise: **I can watch animal populations and vegetation affect each other, and see predation change that living system.**

v0.6 is a supported-scenario ecology stage, not a universal ecosystem simulator. Existing deterministic creature/resource authority is reused; no renderer or survival controller may become a second ecology model.

## Supported release scope

- canonical map size: **24×24**;
- existing/default sandbox behavior remains compatible unless the player explicitly chooses the supported Living Ecology preset;
- validated grazer natural-fauna seed: exactly 10 founders using the Sprint021 vegetation-rich top32 placement + keyed starting ages `[0,6y]`;
- grazer ecology settings for the preset: reproduction `0.001`, gradual old-age mortality enabled, existing hunger/grazing/starvation/resource movement unchanged;
- one additional named predator species only: **Wolf**;
- coherent extinction is allowed; the release does not guarantee long-term coexistence or equilibrium.

## Prior evidence we accept as binding

- Sprint021: exact 10-founder initializer passed 30/30 24×24 seeds for 120 years, with multi-generation reproduction, repeated vegetation pressure/recovery and unchanged sequential RNG;
- larger 32×32/48×48 research does not require founder up-scaling, but those sizes are not part of the v0.6 release promise;
- compact 16×16 initialization remains unresolved and is explicitly outside the canonical gate;
- scalar founder-count tuning is rejected as a universal compact fix;
- single terminal population thresholds are phase-sensitive and are not valid equilibrium gates;
- 40-year low-population recovery envelope failed unseen validation;
- encounter-safe founder placement passed an initial paired study but failed fresh validation (4 rescues vs 6 harms) and is rejected;
- default worlds were intentionally left creature-free by those research sprints, so v0.6 must productize a **supported preset**, not silently reinterpret old evidence as universal defaults.

## Drift guards / non-goals

- No universal 16×16–48×48 natural-fauna initializer.
- No guaranteed ecology equilibrium, minimum species population or persistence controller.
- No hidden creature rescue/reseed after initialization.
- No disease, seasons, weather/climate model, genetics/evolution or plant-species simulation.
- No generalized N-species food-web graph/rules engine.
- No economy/trade/religion/technology/civilization-depth expansion.
- No new god-power breadth.
- No v0.5 Chronicle/AI/replay expansion unless a concrete ecology regression blocks the v0.6 gate.
- No more than three consecutive rejected ecology hypotheses that fail to unlock the visible gate; after that, narrow supported scope instead of extending research indefinitely.

## Product stop rule

Do not add another ecology mechanic merely because it is plausible. A slice enters v0.6 only if it directly improves the supported player's ability to answer:

**What species are here, what are they doing to vegetation and each other, and what happened after predation?**
