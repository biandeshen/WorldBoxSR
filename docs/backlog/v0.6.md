# v0.6 backlog — Living Ecology

Release gate: #223. Version promise: **I can watch animal populations and vegetation affect each other, and see predation change that living system.**

v0.6 is a supported-scenario ecology stage, not a universal ecosystem simulator. Existing deterministic creature/resource authority is reused; no renderer or survival controller may become a second ecology model.

## Supported release scope

- canonical map size: **24×24**;
- existing/default Sandbox remains compatibility-safe unless the player explicitly chooses Living Ecology;
- validated natural Grazer seed: exactly 10 founders using the Sprint021 vegetation-rich top32 placement + keyed `[0,6y]` starting ages;
- Living Ecology Grazer settings: reproduction `0.001`, gradual old-age mortality enabled, existing hunger/grazing/starvation/resource movement unchanged;
- one additional named predator species only: **Wolf**;
- coherent extinction is allowed; no long-term coexistence/equilibrium guarantee.

## Binding prior evidence

- Sprint021: exact 10-founder 24×24 initializer passed 30/30 seeds for 120 years with multi-generation reproduction, repeated vegetation pressure/recovery and unchanged sequential RNG;
- 32×32/48×48 research does not create a v0.6 release promise for those sizes;
- compact 16×16 universal initialization remains unresolved and out of scope;
- scalar founder-count tuning is rejected as a universal compact fix;
- terminal population-at-year-X is phase-sensitive and not an equilibrium gate;
- 40-year low-population recovery envelope failed unseen validation;
- encounter-safe founder placement was rejected after fresh validation produced 4 rescues vs 6 harms;
- therefore v0.6 productizes one supported preset instead of silently redefining all worlds.

## Drift guards / non-goals

- No universal 16×16–48×48 natural-fauna initializer.
- No guaranteed ecology equilibrium, minimum species population or persistence controller.
- No hidden creature rescue/reseed after initialization.
- No disease, seasons, weather/climate, genetics/evolution or plant-species simulation.
- No generalized N-species food-web graph/rules engine.
- No economy/trade/religion/technology/civilization-depth expansion.
- No new God Power breadth.
- No v0.5 Chronicle/AI/replay expansion unless a concrete ecology regression blocks the v0.6 gate.
- No Wolf reproduction or natural-founder policy unless the canonical gate produces concrete evidence that the frozen release promise cannot otherwise be met.
- No more than three consecutive rejected ecology hypotheses that fail to unlock a visible gate; after that, narrow supported scope instead of extending research indefinitely.

## Product stop rule

Do not add an ecology mechanic merely because it is plausible. A slice enters v0.6 only if it directly improves the supported player's ability to answer:

**What species are here, what are they doing to vegetation and each other, and what happened after predation?**
