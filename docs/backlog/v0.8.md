## Semantic boundaries

- `parental_union` stays historical co-parent identity; it does not become marriage, spouse, household, exclusivity, inheritance or political alliance.
- existing maternal `lineage` stays a lineage primitive; it does not become a noble house/dynasty.
- **ruling line** is political identity derived from explicit parent→child descent plus recorded polity succession.
- retained genealogy may resolve descendants after dead ancestors leave current humans; no second ancestry database is introduced.
- succession selection consumes no RNG.
- no eligible descendant preserves the existing deterministic open-selection fallback; no heir is manufactured.
