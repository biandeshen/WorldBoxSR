# v1.1 backlog — Settlement Life & Food Reserves

Direction gate: #326.

Primary player fantasy:

> **A settlement turns the food around it into visible reserves; residents can survive short local shortages from those reserves, and the player can watch scarcity or recovery emerge from the same ecology and God interventions.**

**Status: PLANNED — implementation not yet started.** v1.1 is one finite material-life loop, not a general economy framework.

## Why this stage

Post-1.0 audit scored the candidate families:
- Settlement material life / food reserves: **8.5/10**;
- conflict consequence depth: **7.85/10**;
- terrain/biome shaping: **7.6/10**;
- culture/technology/religion identity: **5.95/10**.

The winning direction has unusually strong existing leverage:
- passable tiles already own authoritative `food` / `foodCapacity` and deterministic regrowth;
- Humans already own hunger/starvation and direct tile-food consumption;
- Settlements already own member population and deterministic territory through `ownerSettlementId`;
- Rain already restores tile food/vegetation and Meteor already damages vegetation;
- Inspector, settlement rendering and World Stories already exist as product surfaces.

What is missing is one authoritative material layer between local land and settlement survival: **stored food**.

## Capability 1 — Settlement Food Reserves + visible Granary — FIRST

**Goal:** make settlement material life visible immediately while conserving existing world food.

### Authority

- one authoritative settlement field: stored food/reserve;
- reserve capacity derived deterministically from existing settlement population/state rather than a second building simulation;
- deterministic territory harvest only moves food from settlement-owned tiles into the reserve — it never creates food;
- harvest may take only bounded surplus above a protected tile-food floor so the settlement cannot vacuum its ecology empty for free;
- hungry settlement members may draw from their home reserve while they remain in the settlement’s local territory before leaving to seek wild tile food;
- no new RNG required for harvesting or reserve draw.

Because the reserve is authoritative and must survive save/load, this capability may increment the engine snapshot schema. If it does, v1.0’s v10–v16 support floor remains mandatory and the new current version must join the compatibility matrix.

### Player-visible surface

- settlement map rendering includes a compact **Granary / reserve** visual whose fill state comes from authority;
- Settlement Inspector exposes stored / capacity and a concise state such as low / stable / full;
- default public world must visibly contain at least two distinguishable reserve levels through ordinary simulation or a bounded deterministic browser setup;
- no separate economy dashboard.

### Acceptance

- [ ] harvesting conserves total food: every amount added to reserve is removed from owned tile food;
- [ ] protected local tile-food floor remains intact;
- [ ] reserve never exceeds deterministic capacity or drops below zero;
- [ ] inactive settlements cannot harvest/draw;
- [ ] hungry local members can consume reserve and the exact amount is conserved;
- [ ] nonmembers / members outside the allowed local territory cannot consume another settlement’s reserve;
- [ ] duplicate runs and save/load continuation remain exact;
- [ ] snapshot migration preserves the v1.0 historical support floor if schema changes;
- [ ] map granary + Inspector read the same authoritative reserve facts;
- [ ] first implementation PR includes real Chromium evidence of visibly different reserve states;
- [ ] no currency, market, trade, jobs or production chain.

## Capability 2 — Scarcity & recovery readability/history

**Goal:** make reserve pressure understandable without turning food into an accounting dashboard.

- record only meaningful reserve transitions (for example healthy→low, depleted, low/depleted→recovered), not every harvest/meal;
- Chronicle/Event Card/World Stories language must state recorded reserve facts only;
- map/Inspector distinguish healthy, low and depleted reserves without inventing prices, class or political motive;
- Rain/Meteor may influence reserve history only through their existing truthful effect on local food/vegetation and subsequent normal harvest/draw behavior;
- no direct “Rain adds X to granary” shortcut unless authority actually transfers that food.

Acceptance:
- [ ] transition events are bounded and non-spammy;
- [ ] cause/history links do not invent economic semantics;
- [ ] presentation remains read-only;
- [ ] scarcity/recovery is legible in the public world.

## Capability 3 — Canonical Settlement Life gate

**Goal:** prove one coherent material-life journey and freeze v1.1.

Canonical shape:
1. deterministic settlement has an authoritative reserve and visible Granary;
2. local harvest transfers surplus tile food into reserve;
3. a bounded shortage/pressure causes resident reserve drawdown;
4. recorded scarcity state becomes visible/readable;
5. environmental/God recovery restores local source food through existing authority;
6. normal harvest replenishes reserve;
7. scarcity→recovery facts remain truthful through Inspector/Story;
8. duplicate and save/load continuation remain exact;
9. all v0.4–v1.0 historical browser/support/Stable-Sandbox regressions remain green.

The gate should reuse existing Rain/Meteor/Story/save infrastructure rather than creating a second economy scenario engine.

## Explicit non-goals

v1.1 does **not** add:
- currency, prices or markets;
- trade routes or inter-settlement transfer;
- professions/classes/jobs;
- taxes, wages, ownership or property;
- crafting or production chains;
- warehouses beyond the single food reserve abstraction;
- technology tree;
- religion/culture;
- economic diplomacy;
- merchant AI;
- a generic resource framework for wood/stone/ore in this version.

## Execution discipline

1. capabilities execute in order;
2. Capability 1 must create a visibly changed public-demo settlement in its first PR;
3. normal slices use focused tests + current PR browser smoke;
4. full historical proof belongs at capability/release boundaries;
5. preserve v1.0 compatibility/support contracts unless explicitly versioned;
6. do not open Capability 2/3 implementation in parallel with Capability 1.
