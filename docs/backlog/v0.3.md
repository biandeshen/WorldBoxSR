# v0.3 backlog — Civilizations Rise

Release gate: #181. The version promise is narrow: **watch small settlements become distinct rival powers and follow at least one political/conflict transition directly on the map.**

## Ordered slices

### 1. Authoritative polity identity — #182
- [ ] stable polity id/name/capital/membership;
- [ ] authoritative color/banner identity;
- [ ] settlement→polity link and lifecycle history;
- [ ] snapshot/save-load migration;
- [ ] Phaser labels/flags/territory driven by polity state;
- [ ] canonical seed45 visibly contains multiple powers.

### 2. Ruler and succession
- [ ] one authoritative ruler per active polity;
- [ ] deterministic eligibility/selection without hidden narrative invention;
- [ ] succession on ruler death;
- [ ] ruler visible in inspector/map/history.

### 3. Relations and diplomacy state
- [ ] pairwise neutral/friendly/hostile relation foundation;
- [ ] bounded causes for relation changes;
- [ ] explicit war/peace state and causal events;
- [ ] readable map/inspector presentation.

### 4. Visible conflict
- [ ] intentionally simple army/combat abstraction;
- [ ] movement/engagement readable on the map;
- [ ] no sophisticated tactical simulator in v0.3.

### 5. Political outcomes
- [ ] settlement conquest/transfer or destruction;
- [ ] polity dissolution when it loses all viable settlements;
- [ ] one bounded rebellion seed mechanic;
- [ ] authoritative territory follows ownership.

### 6. Civilization story surface
- [ ] founding/ruler/war/peace/conquest/rebellion events in causal history;
- [ ] world-event pulse surfaces high-value political transitions;
- [ ] canonical “two powers rise and collide” scenario;
- [ ] executable product gate and final Chromium evidence.

## Stop rules

- No trade/economy/religion/technology unless required for the smallest conflict loop.
- No naval warfare or sophisticated tactics.
- No ecology research or survival tuning.
- No broad art/audio rewrite.
- Every slice must change the public product or establish authoritative truth directly required by the next visible slice.
