## Stop rules

- No new god powers or v0.4 breadth.
- No ecology/species research.
- No economy/trade/religion/technology/civilization-depth expansion.
- No full replay/rewind engine.
- No graph database or generalized knowledge system.
- No AI-authored canonical facts, motives or causal links.
- Optional AI summary is not a blocker and is deferred unless deterministic story presentation proves insufficient.
- Presentation focus/bookmark/navigation state never enters authoritative world snapshots.
