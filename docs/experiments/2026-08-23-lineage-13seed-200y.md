# Lineage structural baseline — 13 seeds × 200 years

Date: 2026-08-23  
Issue: #34  
Seeds: 1–10, 45, 80, 98  
World: 24×24, 30 founders, 200 simulated years

## Purpose

Measure the history-only lineage layer after correcting the original `household` terminology. This is not a behavior experiment: lineage identity does not affect movement, reproduction, food, settlements, or territory.

## Results

Across all 13 worlds:

- founder lineages: 30 in every world;
- extinct-lineage share: min 63.3%, median 73.3%, mean 74.9%, max 90.0%;
- orphan share among living humans: min 16.4%, median 26.9%, mean 30.3%, max 52.4%;
- maximum generation: 8–10, median 9;
- average living lineage size: median 20.3;
- average historical lineage size: median 98.27;
- maximum living lineage size: min 70, median 211, max 379.

For ordinary seeds 1–10, year-200 population median is 624 and extinct-lineage share median remains 73.3%. The high extinction/concentration pattern is therefore not an artifact of the deliberately unusual seed 45.

## Relationships

Within this small structural sample:

- population vs extinct-lineage share: correlation ≈ **0.10** overall;
- population vs orphan share: correlation ≈ **-0.09** overall;
- population vs maximum living lineage size: correlation ≈ **0.93** overall.

The last relationship is expected mechanically: larger populations permit larger surviving lineages. It is not evidence that large lineages cause population growth.

Extinct-lineage share and orphan share do **not** show a clean relationship with total population in this sample. Seed 98, the only sampled world with an abandoned settlement, is not extreme in lineage extinction. Lineage structure therefore does not yet explain settlement abandonment strongly enough to justify attaching behavior to lineage identity.

## Decision

1. **Keep lineages history-only.** They are valuable for ancestry, dynasties, notable families, and future political history.
2. **Do not use lineage as a residential household.** Surviving lineages often contain hundreds of living descendants.
3. **Do not add lineage fertility, food, movement, or inheritance bonuses yet.** The current probe does not support them.
4. The next evidence step is spatial: measure whether close kin (especially parent/child and siblings) actually remain co-located or nearby under the current movement model. A residential household model is justified only if a separate co-residence unit would explain a measurable structural gap.

## Demographic guard

Seed 45 remains at year 200:

- population 128;
- births 184;
- deaths 86;
- food remaining >96%.

The lineage instrumentation remains behavior-neutral.
