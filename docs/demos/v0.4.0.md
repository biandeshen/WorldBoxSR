# v0.4.0 canonical demo — God Power Sandbox

This is the release QA path for the public browser build. It verifies the v0.4 promise: **direct intervention is immediately legible, authoritative, consequential and historically recoverable.**

## Open the product

Public demo: `https://biandeshen.github.io/WorldBoxSR/play/`

Expected default path:
- Phaser renderer;
- seed `45`;
- 24×24 world;
- 30 human founders;
- deterministic warmup to year 40;
- selected speed `60 days` per simulation step;
- six visible God Powers;
- simulation/commands remain authoritative; targeting, FX/SFX and story copy are projections.

Do not change the seed during canonical release QA.

## 5-second god-hand gate

Once `showcase ready` appears, a first-time viewer should be able to identify:

- the living world and its settlements/political identities;
- pause/time/world controls;
- a compact God Powers dock with **Human, Grazer, Erase, Lightning, Meteor and Rain**;
- keyboard hints `1` through `6`;
- current selected-power readout.

The dock should feel immediate, not like a progression/configuration screen.

## Pause the world

Press **Pause** before the canonical intervention sequence.

Confirm the button changes to the paused state and the world year stops advancing. The destructive and restorative evidence should occur on the same frozen authoritative world so natural simulation ticks cannot explain the resource delta.

## Meteor gate

Select **Meteor** (`5`). Hover a populated/vegetated land patch.

Confirm before clicking:
- the target preview is a visible radius-2 square footprint (5×5 maximum);
- the center is distinguished from the surrounding footprint;
- the footprint clips naturally near world bounds;
- water does not invalidate Meteor.

Click once.

Confirm immediately:
- the impact is materially stronger than Lightning;
- blast/ring/particle feedback appears at the actual clicked center;
- the toast truthfully reports affected lives and vegetation removed, or explicitly reports no effect on an empty target;
- impacted humans/grazers disappear because the authoritative command killed them;
- impacted vegetation visibly falls to zero;
- the world remains paused.

## Rain recovery gate

Without unpausing, select **Rain** (`6`) and target the **same center**.

Confirm before clicking:
- the target footprint is the same radius-2 geometry as Meteor;
- the preview is visually constructive/blue rather than destructive;
- water does not invalidate the center, while only passable footprint tiles can receive resources.

Click once.

Confirm immediately:
- Rain has distinct blue rain + green growth feedback and no camera shake;
- the toast reports vegetation and food restored, or explicitly reports that the area is already saturated;
- vegetation visibly returns on the same damaged patch;
- dead humans/grazers do **not** reappear;
- the world remains paused.

For the canonical automated seed45 evidence, the real browser sequence at tile `15,13` records:
- vegetation `189.077 → 0 → 189.077 / 189.077`;
- food `163.212 → 165.464 / 165.464`;
- all 25 passable target tiles at capacity after Rain.

Exact numeric values are evidence for the canonical run, not a UI requirement for manual QA.

## Accepted / no-effect / rejected truth gate

The release distinguishes three concepts rather than treating every click as generic success:

- **applied**: an accepted authoritative action changed the state described by its contract;
- **no effect**: an accepted Meteor/Rain action found nothing to destroy/restore;
- **rejected**: invalid input such as land-only creation on impassable terrain is rejected before command identity is allocated.

Manual spot checks:
- Rain an already-saturated patch and confirm the no-effect wording;
- try Human or Grazer on water and confirm truthful rejection;
- do not expect Meteor/Rain water centers to be rejected.

## World Chronicle gate

Open **Chronicle** after the paused Meteor→Rain sequence.

The most recent direct interventions should be recoverable in order:
1. `Rain renews <x>,<y>`;
2. `Meteor devastates <x>,<y>`.

The Chronicle may also retain representative civilization history, but frequent autonomous events must not erase the short direct intervention story immediately.

Direct player actions should **not** appear as autonomous world-event pulses. The ordinary direct-action toast is their immediate feedback surface.

## Headless deterministic gate

`client/presentation/god_power_gate.js` is the reusable non-browser product proof. It constructs a controlled world and invokes the real engine Meteor and Rain commands.

The test gate requires:
1. in-footprint life/resources before intervention;
2. real Meteor mortality and vegetation removal;
3. unchanged food after Meteor;
4. exact Rain restoration to existing capacities;
5. no resurrection;
6. stable terrain/climate fields and capacities;
7. unchanged sequential RNG;
8. ordered `god.meteor` then `god.rain` history;
9. byte-identical duplicate evidence and final snapshots.

## God-sandbox regression gate

v0.4 must preserve the rest of the playable world:
- Human/Grazer creation remains authoritative and land-only;
- Erase/Lightning remain usable;
- pan, zoom, pause, time speed, world reset and inspection remain usable;
- civilization/ruler/war/conquest history from v0.3 remains truthful;
- reduced-motion/reduced-flash profiles still reduce generated feedback.


Required release evidence:
- merged-main CI success;
- merged-main production Pages success;

- release PR's own CI/Chromium success;
- release workflow successfully creates tag `v0.4.0` and GitHub release from `docs/releases/v0.4.0.md`;
- final Pages workflow verifies the public `/play/` URL serves the game.

## Release question

After automated gates are green, the release boundary is:

> Can I choose a destructive or restorative god power, understand exactly where it will act, immediately see and feel the authoritative consequence, distinguish a real effect from no effect/rejection, and recover what I did from the world's history?

A yes closes v0.4. Do not add a seventh power during release handoff; v0.5 shifts active product development to **World Stories**.
