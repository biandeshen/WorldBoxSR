## Semantic truth gate

The following claims are explicitly out of scope and must not appear merely because a ruling line exists:
- marriage/spouse/household;
- noble house;
- legitimacy or blood-right claim;
- primogeniture;
- election;
- usurpation;
- political motive.

`parental_union` remains historical co-parent identity. Existing maternal `lineage` remains a different lineage primitive. Ruling-line continuation comes only from explicit parent→child descent plus recorded polity succession facts.
