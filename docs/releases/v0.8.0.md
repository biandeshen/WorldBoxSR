# WorldBoxSR v0.8.0 — Ruling Lines & Succession

v0.8.0 deepens the existing civilization simulation through one bounded political seam:

> **I can watch a ruling bloodline inherit power across generations, see when that bloodline loses the throne, and follow the new ruling line in the same causal world history.**

This release does not add marriage, noble houses, claims, elections or a general politics framework. A **ruling line** is a minimal political identity derived from explicit parent→child descent and recorded succession facts.

## Shipped scope

### Genealogical succession resolver

WorldBoxSR now has a pure, cycle-safe, RNG-neutral descendant resolver over already-authoritative parent/child records.

- current Human `parentIds` plus retained parental-union child records form the ancestry graph;
- dead intermediate ancestors remain traversable without resurrecting entities;
- paternal and maternal descent both work;
- deterministic rank = nearest generation → older age → lower stable Human ID;
- `parental_union` remains historical co-parent identity, not marriage/household/spouse semantics;
- existing maternal `lineage` remains a separate lineage primitive, not a dynasty/house.

The frozen seed45 audit found a real behavior-changing opportunity at day `9150` / Y25.4167: Eldergate Realm #1 ruler/founder Human #23 had eligible direct child Human #31 while the old open-selection rule would have chosen Human #28.

### Authoritative ruling-line succession

Polity succession now follows one deterministic rule:

1. founding ruler remains the existing oldest-eligible-adult appointment;
2. later vacancies first choose the best eligible descendant of the current ruling-line founder;
3. if no eligible descendant exists, the existing oldest-adult open-selection fallback remains unchanged and starts a new ruling line;
4. no succession RNG is introduced.

The polity persists only the minimal political identity required for deterministic continuation: founder ID, line sequence, line start day and reign count. Snapshot schema v16 migrates older polity state deterministically without inventing historical events or dates.

### Ruling-line Inspector readability

The existing Phaser Inspector now exposes compact factual context for current rulers and polity settlements:

- ruling-line number;
- reign count;
- founder Human #ID;
- retained transition wording such as `bloodline continued · child` or `new ruling line · open selection`.

The projection is read-only. Missing founders remain stable IDs rather than being inferred or replaced.

### Dynastic World Stories

Existing Event Cards and the Rule Chronicle lens now render already-recorded ruler transitions as readable political history.

Examples from the production browser gate:

- `Eldergate Realm's ruling bloodline continues` — Human #31 continues recorded ruling line 67 as a child of founder Human #23;
- `Lindenvale Dominion begins a new ruling line` — Human #11 begins recorded ruling line 28 after an open-selection fallback.

Event subject/cause resolution, Rule membership/order and map/event navigation remain the existing authoritative paths. Presentation never invents legitimacy, primogeniture, claims, elections, usurpation or political motive.

The final v0.8 composition gate proves that the individual slices form one trustworthy player journey.

## Compatibility note

v0.8 intentionally changes deterministic political history after succession becomes descendant-aware. Published v0.7 release identities and evidence remain immutable. The Scenario Recipe/share/Replay/Fork contract remains deterministic, while current-main world fingerprints may differ from v0.7 because authoritative simulation behavior has legitimately changed.

## Deliberate non-goals

v0.8 does **not** add:
- economy/trade/storage/currency;
- professions/classes;
- marriage/spouse/household or marriage diplomacy;
- noble-house graphs, titles, claims or legitimacy;
- configurable succession laws, elections or claimant civil wars;
- religion/culture/technology;
- boats/naval warfare;
- broad diplomacy rewrite;
- terrain/editor expansion;
- AI-authored political facts;
- fertility/migration rescue or a controller that guarantees dynasty survival.

Natural ruling-line failure remains valid emergence.
