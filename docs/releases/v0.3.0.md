# WorldBoxSR v0.3.0 — Civilizations Rise

v0.3.0 turns settlements into **visible political actors whose histories can be followed on the map**. The release deliberately stops at the smallest coherent civilization loop: powers form, real human rulers lead them, relations can turn into war or peace, authoritative warbands move and fight, settlements can change hands or rebel, and the Chronicle explains those transitions from recorded causal history.

The goal is not to become a grand-strategy game. Civilization mechanics exist because they create visible autonomous world stories inside the god sandbox.

## Shipped

### Political identity

- active settlements form deterministic named polities with stable IDs;
- polity capitals, colors and banner styles are authoritative simulation state;
- settlement territory projects the controlling polity without inventing ownership in Phaser;
- polity founding and dissolution are recorded in causal history.

### Real rulers and succession

- each viable polity selects a real living adult human as ruler;
- selection is deterministic: oldest eligible adult first with stable ID tie-breaking;
- ruler death or loss of membership can produce truthful succession or vacancy;
- rulers remain normal humans rather than decorative presentation-only NPCs.

### Relations, war and peace

- every active polity pair owns one authoritative relation;
- proximity, shared borders and bounded keyed political drift change relations without consuming sequential simulation RNG;
- hostile pressure can start war and reduced pressure/war fatigue can produce peace;
- dissolution truthfully closes and archives impossible relations/wars.

### Visible armies and combat

- war creates authoritative warbands derived from living adult population;
- warbands move deterministically through passable space toward enemy settlement objectives;
- spatial engagement produces bounded deterministic strength loss;
- combat does not silently kill human population;
- a deterministic tie rule prevents unusable double-destruction outcomes so a surviving force can continue toward a political result.

### Conquest and bounded rebellion

- an uncontested surviving warband reaching an enemy settlement transfers its political ownership;
- polity membership/capital are recomputed and landless powers dissolve through existing polity authority;
- captured viable non-capital settlements have one bounded deterministic opportunity to rebel after an occupation delay;
- conquest and rebellion state persist in snapshot schema v15 with legacy migration support.

### Civilization story surface

- raw political event names are projected into readable Chronicle headlines and details;
- founding, succession, war/peace, mobilization, battle, conquest, rebellion and dissolution remain derived from authoritative history;
- Chronicle selection deliberately preserves representative conflict/outcome events so frequent ruler churn cannot crowd the actual civilization story out of view;
- high-value world-event pulses use an event cursor so 40 years of warmup history are not replayed as if they just happened;
- presentation may dramatize and summarize recorded facts, but does not invent canonical causes or world state.

## Canonical v0.3 gate

The public default remains deterministic seed `45`, 24×24, 30 founders, warmed to year 40.

An independent release probe over that exact default produced:

- multiple founded powers with real rulers;
- a recorded war;
- multiple authoritative warband engagements;
- a settlement conquest;
- a later rebellion;
- multiple active powers still present at year 40.

The reusable deterministic collision gate also constructs a compact two-power scenario through the real polity/ruler/relation/warband/conquest systems and requires the complete loop to pass twice with byte-identical resulting snapshots.

## Product gates satisfied

### Civilization readability

A player can identify distinct powers through names, flags/colors, rulers, borders and settlement ownership without opening debug metrics.

### Conflict legibility

War state appears in the HUD/inspectors, authoritative warbands are visible on the map, engagements change their strength, and political territory changes when conquest occurs.

### Story legibility

The default showcase Chronicle includes readable high-value history such as rebellion, peace, conquest and army destruction rather than only raw engine event names or repeated succession noise.

### Determinism / truth

- simulation state remains the only authoritative world truth;
- no presentation system consumes hidden simulation RNG or maintains a second political model;
- exact save/load continuation remains covered;
- v10–v14 snapshot compatibility migrates into the current schema;
- CI, production Pages build checks and real Chromium Visual QA remain part of the release gate.

## Known limits / deliberate non-goals

v0.3 stops before civilization depth becomes a separate strategy-game project.

- no deep economy or trade system;
- no technology, religion or culture progression;
- no alliances or sophisticated diplomacy graph;
- no tactical formations, sieges or naval warfare;
- no broad ecology expansion;
- no editor/replay product loop;
- rebellion is intentionally one bounded seed mechanic rather than a full loyalty/civil-war simulation;
- prototype/programmatic visual art remains in place.

These are not missing pieces required to call v0.3 complete; they belong to later evidence-driven stages if the god-sandbox experience needs them.

## What comes next

### v0.4 — God Power Sandbox

Development now returns to the second core product pillar: **a satisfying god hand**. The next stage should make intervention fun and consequential through stronger creation/destruction/social powers, targeting, effects and recorded secondary consequences — without reopening civilization depth or ecology research as blockers.

The project north star remains: **a living pixel god sandbox where worlds visibly grow, collapse, and remember why.**
