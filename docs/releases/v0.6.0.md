# WorldBoxSR v0.6.0 — Living Ecology

v0.6.0 makes ecology a visible playable world system: **I can watch animal populations and vegetation affect each other, and see predation change that living system.**

The release deliberately supports one truthful scope instead of pretending to be a universal ecosystem simulator. The canonical Living Ecology world is 24×24. Its Grazer lifecycle/resource behavior and Wolf predation are authoritative simulation state; the browser renders and explains that state but never owns a second ecology model.

## Shipped

### Supported Living Ecology preset

The Phaser product surface now exposes **Sandbox · Living Ecology**.

- Sandbox remains the compatibility-safe default and keeps its historical showcase behavior.
- Living Ecology starts at day0 with exactly 10 natural Grazer founders.
- Founder placement is the accepted 24×24 vegetation-rich deterministic initializer from prior validation.
- Founder ages use keyed `[0,6y]` values; initialization consumes zero sequential world RNG.
- Natural founders do not create fake `god.spawn_creature` history.
- Living Ecology enables Grazer reproduction `0.001` plus gradual old-age mortality while preserving existing Grazer movement, grazing, hunger and starvation behavior.
- There is no later rescue, reseed, population target or survival controller.

The release does not claim this initializer is universally valid for every compact/larger map size.

### Grazer + Wolf creature surface

The authoritative creature domain and Phaser presentation now support exactly two shipped species identities: **Grazer** and **Wolf**.

- Both use shared creature id/position/age/health/hunger/lifecycle state.
- `spawn_creature` accepts exactly `grazer | wolf`.
- Both species are selectable and inspectable through the same authoritative creature path.
- Grazers retain their orange/brown form; Wolves use a distinct gray silhouette.
- Inspector shows current species, age, health, hunger and tile.
- HUD keeps a compact Grazer count and adds `🐺 N` only when Wolves exist.
- Current creature history/reference navigation uses the existing `entityKind:'creature'` contract.

The hidden Wolf spawn option is release QA/setup plumbing only; v0.6 does not add a new visible Wolf God Power.

### Authoritative Wolf predation

Wolf behavior is deterministic engine authority, not renderer animation.

Frozen v0.6 candidate values passed without release-gate tuning:
- hunger/day `0.01`;
- hungry threshold `0.35`;
- prey search radius `6` Chebyshev cells;
- feed amount `0.70`;
- starvation threshold `0.90`;
- starvation damage/day `0.025`;
- recovery/day `0.004`.

When hungry, a Wolf selects the nearest living Grazer inside its bounded search radius, then the lowest creature ID. It moves at most one passable tile per day toward prey, consumes no sequential RNG, kills at most one range-1 Grazer per day, feeds, and can starve when prey is unavailable.

A successful hunt records explicit causal truth:
1. `creature.predated` — prey as Subject, living Wolf as Cause, predator/prey ids + species, hunt tile and Wolf hunger before/after feeding;
2. matching `creature.died(cause:'predation')` caused by the predation event;
3. current prey removal through the shared creature lifecycle.

There is no attack-probability/damage framework, pack behavior, renderer-owned kill or hidden prey spawn.

### Ecology readability

The release adds only restrained game-facing explanation over authority:

- selected Grazer/Wolf behavior labels are derived from current hunger/config;
- selected creature Inspector reprojects current position/age/health/hunger rather than keeping stale pre-hunt state;
- Living Ecology HUD shows compact `🌿 N%` exactly matching authoritative vegetation utilization;
- `creature.predated` becomes `Wolf #X hunted Grazer #Y` plus recorded hunt tile and hunger before→after;
- predation joins **Recent** Chronicle while v0.5 Highlights, Conflict and Rule policies remain unchanged;
- the existing Causal Event Card shows removed prey truthfully unavailable and the living Wolf Cause map-navigable;
- one restrained World Event Pulse reports a successful hunt.

No heatmap, chart, trend dashboard or inferred ecological motive is introduced.

## Canonical v0.6 gate

The final release gate proves natural fauna, vegetation pressure/recovery, Wolf predation, readable consequence, repeatability and save/load continuity as one supported release path.

### Frozen natural trajectory

A temporary yearly measurement probe was used before the permanent gate and then deleted. The permanent seed45 release gate locks these existing authoritative checkpoints:

- **Y34:** 136 living Grazers · vegetation `34.31%` · 150 natural births;
- **Y40 trough:** 116 Grazers · vegetation `17.50%` · 156 natural births;
- **Y50 recovery:** 68 Grazers · vegetation `37.44%` · 160 natural births.

So the observed world shows:
- Y34 → Y40 vegetation pressure: `-16.81` percentage points;
- Y40 → Y50 vegetation recovery: `+19.94` points while living Grazers fall `116 → 68` through ordinary lifecycle;
- zero hidden god creature spawn/rescue/reseed before the explicit Wolf setup;
- pre-Wolf creature deaths through Y50 recorded as ordinary old age.

This is described as an observed pressure/recovery phase. It is **not** a promise of equilibrium, carrying capacity or eternal coexistence, and the later Wolf is not credited with causing the earlier recovery.

### Frozen Wolf continuation

After Y50 the canonical gate uses one fixed explicit QA Wolf setup at tile `(0,8)`, measured once and then frozen rather than searched at test time.

- canonical Wolf: #171;
- nearest Grazer distance at setup: `3`;
- first Wolf movement: `(0,8) → (1,9)`;
- first canonical prey: Grazer #110;
- only one explicit god creature spawn exists after natural initialization.

### Determinism and save/load

The permanent headless release test runs the complete path three ways:
1. uninterrupted;
2. duplicate;
3. snapshot at Y40, restore, then continue through recovery + Wolf hunt.

All three produce identical evidence and byte-identical final authoritative snapshots. Existing ecology Story/Event Card/Pulse projections agree with the recorded hunt without mutating snapshot or RNG.

### Real browser release path

Full production-build Chromium reproduces the same facts through actual product controls:

1. choose **Living Ecology** and Reset;
2. land paused at exact Y40 with `🐾116 · 🌿18%`;
3. use ordinary **1 year** Time + Play to reach exact Y50 `🐾68 · 🌿37%`;
4. real-click the fixed QA Wolf setup tile `(0,8)` once;
5. use ordinary **1 day** Time + Play;
6. Wolf #171 moves to `(1,9)` and authority records the hunt of Grazer #110;
7. Pulse, Recent and Event Card explain `Wolf #171 hunted Grazer #110`;
8. dead prey is unavailable, living Wolf Cause navigates back to the current Wolf;
9. current Inspector shows the post-feed Wolf state;
10. all paused Recent/Event Card/map/inspection actions leave the serialized authoritative world unchanged.

Three fixed 1440×900 release screenshots preserve the Y40 trough, Y50 recovery and readable post-predation state. Manual review confirms the map remains the primary product surface.

## Product gates satisfied

### Ecology is real simulation authority

Grazer lifecycle/resource use and Wolf hunting live in deterministic engine systems. Renderer/query code does not decide who eats, moves, reproduces, dies or survives.

### Natural population change affects a visible landscape

The supported seed45 world demonstrates a material vegetation drawdown followed by later recovery while Grazer abundance falls through ordinary lifecycle.

### Predation is explicit and causal

The Wolf→Grazer consequence is recorded in event/death history and reflected by current authority, not inferred later from proximity.

### A first-time player can understand the consequence

Compact species counts, current creature behavior, `🌿 N%`, Pulse, Recent and the existing Event Card expose enough truth without raw JSON or an analytics dashboard.

### Save/load and repeatability remain exact

The canonical release path is byte-repeatable and a Y40 save→load continuation reaches the same Y50 recovery and post-hunt authoritative result as uninterrupted simulation.

## Known limits / deliberate non-goals

v0.6 stops at one supported ecology slice.

- no universal 16×16–48×48 natural-fauna initializer;
- no equilibrium, minimum-species or long-term coexistence guarantee;
- no hidden carrying-capacity/population controller or rescue/reseed;
- no natural Wolf founder policy;
- no Wolf reproduction, mating, packs or territory system;
- no additional predator/prey species or generic food-web/rules framework;
- no disease, seasons, climate/weather, genetics/evolution or plant-species simulation;
- no ecology heatmap/analytics dashboard;
- no new visible God Power;
- no renewed economy/trade/religion/technology/civilization-depth expansion;
- no reopening v0.5 story breadth beyond ecology integration needed by this release.

Coherent decline or extinction remains valid when it follows authority and invariants.

## What comes next

v0.6 feature breadth is frozen at this release boundary. v0.7 planning may begin only after tag/Release/Pages/Chromium publication verification is complete.

The roadmap currently reserves v0.7 for **World Builder & Scenarios**, but its exact finite scope must be planned from the shipped product state rather than smuggled into this release handoff.

The project north star remains: **a living pixel god sandbox where worlds visibly grow, collapse, and remember why.**
