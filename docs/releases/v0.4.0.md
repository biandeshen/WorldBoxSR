# WorldBoxSR v0.4.0 — God Power Sandbox

v0.4.0 strengthens the second core WorldBoxSR fantasy: **intervening in the world should feel satisfying immediately, while the consequences remain authoritative and historically traceable.**

The release deliberately stays small. It does not add a catalog of disasters or a progression system. Instead it proves one destructive area power and one constructive area power all the way through the real product loop:

`choose → understand target → act → see/feel impact → inspect authoritative aftermath → recover the intervention from world history`.

## Shipped

### Six-power God Powers dock

The browser product now intentionally stops at six visible powers for v0.4:

1. Human;
2. Grazer;
3. Erase;
4. Lightning;
5. Meteor;
6. Rain.

Labels, keyboard shortcuts, target radius/style and passability requirements share one presentation metadata source. The dock remains an immediate sandbox control surface rather than becoming a configuration/progression UI.

### Meteor — authoritative area destruction

- fixed Chebyshev radius `2` (5×5 maximum), clipped at world bounds;
- exact footprint is previewed before the click;
- valid over land or water centers;
- living humans and grazers in the footprint die through normal lifecycle authority;
- impacted passable vegetation is cleared;
- food, terrain/passability, climate fields, capacities and political state are not directly mutated;
- empty strikes are accepted truthful `no_effect` actions rather than fake success or rejection;
- generated impact audio, blast rings, particles and camera feedback are materially stronger than Lightning;
- reduced-motion/reduced-flash preferences sharply reduce motion/flash and remove camera shake;
- `god.meteor` plus causal death events preserve why the damage happened.

### Rain — authoritative area restoration

- uses the same fixed radius-2 target contract;
- only passable impacted tiles are resource targets;
- existing `vegetation` and `food` restore to each tile's already-authoritative capacities;
- Rain does not edit moisture, fertility, biome, terrain, capacities, entity identity or political state;
- Rain never resurrects life killed by Meteor;
- saturated areas are accepted truthful `no_effect` actions;
- constructive generated rain/growth FX and audio are visually and sonically distinct from destructive powers and do not shake the camera;
- `god.rain` records the exact vegetation/food delta.

Rain is a magical direct intervention, **not** a weather, hydrology or ecology simulation.

### Truthful action outcomes

Accepted browser god actions now share a small presentation outcome contract:

- `applied` — the accepted action produced its authoritative effect;
- `no_effect` — the action was accepted but the authoritative target state meant nothing changed.

Rejected invalid input remains validation/exception based and still allocates no command identity. Presentation does not invent a separate rejection/world-state model.

### World Chronicle remembers the god hand

Direct interventions are projected from authoritative history into readable entries such as:

- `Meteor devastates 15,13`;
- `Rain renews 15,13`.

The Chronicle preserves the two most recent direct god interventions alongside representative autonomous civilization history, so a player can recover a short cause-and-response sequence. Direct actions do not masquerade as autonomous world-event pulses.

## Canonical v0.4 gates

### Real Chromium interaction gate

The production-site Visual QA now pauses the deterministic default seed45 showcase at year 40 and drives the actual browser with real pointer input:

1. select Meteor;
2. capture the exact radius preview;
3. click a populated/vegetated tile through Phaser input;
4. require an authoritative `god.meteor` event plus truthful impact toast;
5. select Rain on the same center;
6. capture the same radius preview;
7. click through Phaser input;
8. require authoritative `god.rain`, exact resource deltas and full capacity restoration;
9. open Chronicle and require Rain followed by Meteor.

The canonical evidence at tile `15,13` records:

- vegetation `189.077 → 0 → 189.077 / 189.077`;
- food `163.212 → 165.464 / 165.464`;
- all 25 passable footprint tiles saturated after Rain;
- the world remains paused during the sequence.

### Headless deterministic product gate

`client/presentation/god_power_gate.js` builds a compact controlled world and invokes the real engine commands directly. The gate requires:

- in-footprint life and vegetation before intervention;
- real Meteor mortality + vegetation removal;
- Meteor leaves food unchanged;
- Rain restores food/vegetation exactly to existing capacities;
- Rain does not resurrect life;
- terrain/climate signature and capacities remain unchanged;
- sequential world RNG remains unchanged;
- `god.meteor` precedes `god.rain` in authoritative history;
- duplicate runs from identical pre-state produce byte-identical evidence and final snapshots.

## Product gates satisfied

### Immediate intervention readability

A player can choose one of six powers, see the actual target footprint before an area action, click the world and get immediate visual/audio feedback without opening debug tools.

### Consequence truth

Meteor and Rain mutate only the authoritative state described by their contracts. Presentation reads those outcomes; it does not simulate a second result.

### Accepted/no-effect honesty

Empty Meteor strikes and saturated Rain strikes are explicitly distinguishable from successful state-changing actions. Invalid land-only creation input remains a real rejection.

### Causal memory

The world remembers the direct intervention itself and normal downstream systems remain responsible for any later settlement/civilization consequences.

### Accessibility / delivery

Generated feedback remains covered by reduced-motion/reduced-flash tests, and CI + production Pages build checks + real Chromium interaction QA remain part of the release gate.

## Known limits / deliberate non-goals

v0.4 deliberately does **not** grow into a generalized disaster or ecology project.

- no fire propagation;
- no plague or tornado system;
- no crater/terrain deformation from Meteor;
- no weather duration, clouds, runoff, rivers or hydrology from Rain;
- no moisture/fertility editor or biome succession;
- no species survival controller or ecology balancing research;
- no cooldown, mana, inventory or power progression;
- no new civilization/economy/religion/technology depth;
- no terrain editor or replay/rewind;
- no seventh power in this release.

## What comes next

### v0.5 — World Stories

The next stage shifts from adding intervention breadth to helping players **follow, revisit and remember what the world did**: richer game-first Chronicle/event cards, causal drill-down, bookmarks/favorites, polity/ruler/settlement story views and timeline navigation. Optional AI summaries, if explored, must remain strictly derived from recorded authoritative facts.

The project north star remains: **a living pixel god sandbox where worlds visibly grow, collapse, and remember why.**
